How the ballot works.

  1. Entries close at 23:59 on Sunday 2 May 2027.

  2. The draw is made in public at the Trust's spring meeting in the turnstile hall on Saturday 8 May 2027. 100 places are drawn from Wrenmouth postcodes first; the other 300 are drawn from everyone, including the Wrenmouth names that missed out.

  3. Everyone who entered gets an email on 8 May saying yes or no.

  4. If you can't come, give your place back by 24 May. On 25 May the returned places are drawn again from the names left in the hat.

  5. Bring the email on the morning, on your phone or on paper. Places aren't transferable; the lifeguards need to know who's in the water.

What it'll be like.

Cold, crowded and loud, for half an hour. Then tea.

  • The water will be about 14 °C. That's cold enough to take your breath for the first minute. Go in down the steps at the shallow end, slowly, and breathe out.
  • Lifeguards: twelve on the side and two in the water. The first-timers' end is the cascade end, where it's shallowest.
  • Bring a swimsuit, a towel, a hat for afterwards, warm layers, and something on your feet for the walk along the wall.
  • Getting here at 05:15 is easier on foot or by bike. The first 41 bus arrives at 05:40. Directions are on the visit page.
  • Didn't get a place? The sea wall is public. Come and watch, and swim from 09:00 when the lido opens to everyone.
