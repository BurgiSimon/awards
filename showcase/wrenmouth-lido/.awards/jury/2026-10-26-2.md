Home desktop-s00 shares nothing compositional with White Desert (full-bleed film, giant condensed caps, drawn grid), Son Daven (render-led poster, dithered wordmark, metric pair) or Eugeniagrab (3D plants on paper). One near-miss for the record: the concrete ground `#E7E5DF` (`src/styles/tokens.css:8`) is within two units of White Desert's `#E9E7E1`. With the slate sea ground and one warm action colour, this sits close to the "navy-ink-on-bone + orange set" the DIVERGENCE block refused, and `DESIGN.md:106` takes that card's accent rule on purpose. It is not nameable from the frame because the ink is near-black and the accent is brick red, not orange.
competitor: Jubilee Pool, Penzance (a 1935 seawater tidal lido on the sea front, community-owned and restored), named from the subject and place before the contract's line was weighed — breaks. Jubilee has decades of its own photographs and no dated reopening morning, so an empty frame stamped '27 6 14 with 400 ballot places cannot serve it. The inventory item the page leaves to text alone is the tide mechanism itself, the sluice and the spring-tide refill: the signature never touches it. The stepped cascade fountain also appears only as a sentence. The contract's own competitor (Tinside) is of similar difficulty; it names Grange Lido as the one that could borrow the empty frame, which is honest.
- white-desert — image resolution at full bleed: White Desert's full-bleed film and ice plates hold detail at 1440 (white-desert desktop-s00, desktop-s50), while the sea plate here is a 1280 px source stretched to 1440 and visibly soft in its waves and mountains (gaps desktop-s10). Recorded under Exceptions `P09@desktop` with a reason, so it is not on the fix list.
- son-daven — finish of the one made image: Son Daven's hero object carries three levels of detail from the building down to the balcony rails (son-daven desktop-s00). The empty frame, the build's only made image and the only picture in the first viewport on four routes, reads at 1440 as a flat grey-teal fill with corners and a stamp; its grain (`src/styles/main.css:150`, noise alpha .16) does not survive the screen (dawn-swim, season-passes, trust and visit desktop-s00). This enters Craft as fix 2.
- eugeniagrab — neighbour frame unavailable (403 on recapture; card §3/§8 used). Its fixed frame repeated per object changes the object each time. Here the repeated frame changes only a five-character stamp ('27 6 14 / '28 3 5 / No. 2401 / 60 m), so across routes the frame reads as repetition rather than sequence (dawn-swim, season-passes, trust and visit desktop-s00).
