What's in it.

A pass is for one named person, from 14 June 2027 to 13 June 2028.

  • Every public session, every day the lido's open: 06:30 to 20:00 in summer, 07:00 to 17:00 in winter.
  • Early lanes for pass holders from 06:00 on weekdays, May to September.
  • The sauna at £3 a session instead of £5.
  • Not included: the opening dawn swim (that's the free ballot), swim school courses, and the Christmas morning swim, which raises money for the lifeboat.
  • Refill mornings: twice a month the pool closes for about four hours while the tide refills it. Pass holders get a text the day before.

Is it worth it?

A single adult swim is £6. Tell us how often you'll come and we'll do the sum.

At 4 swims a month, an adult pass works out at £3.02 a swim, against £6 for a single. It pays for itself after 25 swims.
