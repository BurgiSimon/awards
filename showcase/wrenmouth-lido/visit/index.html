The water.

Seawater, straight from the bay, never heated. It's refilled by the tide twice a month, on the spring tides, so it's always about the temperature of the sea. Today's reading is chalked on the board by the turnstile at opening.

Typical figures for the bay; a warm week in August or a cold snap in February can move them by two or three degrees. The first-swim times are a rule of thumb for people new to cold water, not a target.

What to bring.

Less than you think in summer. More than you think in winter.

  • A swimsuit and a towel. Two towels from October.
  • Warm layers to put on after, easy ones: you won't be good at buttons for ten minutes.
  • A woolly hat for winter, and a swim cap if you like one.
  • Something on your feet for the walk along the wall. Neoprene boots and gloves are welcome below 12 °C.
  • A £1 coin for the lockers. You get it back.
  • Leave at home: glass, and dogs (assistance dogs are welcome).

Getting here.

The lido is at the end of the sea wall, past the lifeboat station. The turnstile hall is the way in.

On foot
12 minutes from Wrenmouth station: down Station Road, left along the harbour, and keep going until the wall ends.
By bus
The 41 from the county town stops at Harbour Road, three minutes away. First bus 05:40.
By bike
Racks for 60 bikes inside the gate.
By car
Harbour Road car park, pay and display, 120 spaces, four minutes' walk. Blue-badge bays beside the turnstile hall.
Step-free
From Harbour Road through the turnstile hall to the poolside. A pool hoist at the shallow end, and an accessible changing room with a hoist and bench.
