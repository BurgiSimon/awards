Skills

Skills cannot call each other, so each phase ends with a written hand-off to the next. Every skill can also be asked for on its own.

craft
Builds a whole site end to end and owns AWARDS.md. The entry point when you are unsure which skill applies.
concept
Finds the one idea, the story and the signature interaction; deals three directions with a seeded roll and writes the direction contract.
system
Type contract, colour strategy, tokens and themed browser surfaces; writes DESIGN.md and tokens.css.
structure
Page map, hero archetype, components, semantic skeleton, responsive strategy and the 404.
stack
Scaffolds Vite (default), Next, Nuxt, Astro, SvelteKit or Webflow with Lenis, GSAP and Three on one ticker.
motion
Preloader, reveals, scrubs, cursor, transitions and the reduced-motion tiers.
webgl
The GL layer with Three.js, OGL, React Three Fiber or Threlte: shaders, asset pipeline, quality tiers and fallbacks.
component
One award-level component inside an existing site, inheriting its world.
jury
Scores work like a design-award jury in a fresh context and returns ordered fixes and a disposition.
ship
Applies the fixes in one batch, then runs the audit, captures, performance, accessibility and meta checks and writes the ship report.
research
Turns a reference site into a case-study card in the corpus format.

The craft flow

Brief, concept, system, structure, stack, motion, WebGL, jury, ship. Two rules hold throughout: no page code before the direction contract is locked, and no stack chosen before the concept. A phase runs only when its entry condition holds, and its exit artefact is what the next phase reads.

The direction contract has ten blocks: THESIS, WORLD, STORY, FIRST VIEWPORT, SIGNATURE, SCROLL MODEL, LOAD & CLOSE, DIVERGENCE, FINISH and the seed. A mood word in any of them (premium, immersive, cinematic) sends it back to concept.

Jury

The juror runs in a forked context so the build conversation cannot anchor it. It inventories the first viewport in its own words before it reads the contract, then scores four axes on anchored scales plus five developer criteria.

Jury axes and weights
AxisWeight
Design40%
Usability30%
Creativity20%
Content10%
recapture
The evidence is missing, blank, stale or broken. No score exists yet.
rebuild
Weighted score under 6.8, a juror could name the source site, the memory test returns only a mood, or the thesis is not visible.
fix
Score from 6.8 to 7.19, any axis under 6.8, P0 or P1 audit findings, or a broken contract row.
ship
7.2 or more, no axis under 6.8, no P0 or P1 findings, and the usability walk passed.

Corpus

%%sites%% site cards and %%patterns%% pattern files. Cards are read for their principles and their take and don't-take lists; the rest is evidence, never a plan. Every direction contract names three neighbouring cards, the principle taken from each and the literal move refused.

Scripts

Paths are relative to the plugin folder. The skills call these for you; they also run on their own.

scripts/doctor.mjs <project>
Checks Node, dependencies, Playwright and a real browser launch. Installs nothing.
scripts/audit.mjs <dir|file|url> [--render] [--json]
The craft-floor audit.
scripts/capture.mjs <url|dir> [--reduced-motion] [--no-webgl]
Jury evidence: three scroll positions, a phone, reduced motion and scripted states.
scripts/new-project.mjs --stack vite --name <dir> [--webgl]
Copies the Vite scaffold with one ticker and the capture hook already wired.
scripts/roll.mjs --deal 3 --of 7
The seeded direction roll used by the concept skill.
scripts/verify-recipes.mjs
Builds every recipe and runs its verifier in headless Chromium.
