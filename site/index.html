A plugin for Claude Code and Codex that designs and builds a whole website, then hands the screenshots to a juror in a separate session, who scores it the way an award jury does and usually sends it back.

How a build goes

Nine phases, each ending in a file the next one reads. Skills cannot call each other, so nothing is carried in the conversation: AWARDS.md is the build’s memory, and a new session resumes from it. Under each phase, one line from the Museum of Extinct Colours’ own files.

The craft flow, phase by phase, in the docs

96 recipes, each one running

The parts a build reaches for, from a preloader that waits for a real signal to a page-wide shader, each a small Vite page with its own verifier that drives it in headless Chromium. These are their frames, one each, by group.

  • 96 entries: 95 focused recipes and one complete page; 89 carry a recorded pass.

  • 65 award sites read in depth, every fact on a card labelled verified, recalled, inferred or unknown; 59 with a measured composition skeleton.

  • 27 pattern files that turn the cards into rules, each citing the cards it came from.

The four words it ends on

ship
Weighted 7.2 or higher.

A page is never judged by the session that built it. The juror is forked from the build, reads the screenshots before the contract, and describes the first screen in its own words before it scores anything.

Under it, an audit with no model in the loop: 95 rules in eight families, the quick ones after every edit and all of them on the rendered page at three widths. 23 of them look for what a generator does when nobody decided, and two such habits left on a page cap Creativity at 6.

The plugin holds itself to the same test. Version 1.0.0 read its own five finished builds and named what they shared as a rut of its own, which every new concept now has to refuse.
