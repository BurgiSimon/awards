PASS if the reply carries an ordered list of fixes — the skill's reply contract asks for the top
three, and up to eight is fine — each naming a concrete location (a file, a selector, a section or
an audit rule id) and a change, and if the reply ANYWHERE, inside that list or in the reasoning
around it, names at least two of these problems: reflex fonts (Inter / Space Grotesk), gradient
text, the glass or blob hero, the hero-metric row, the identical card grid, generic CTA copy such as
Get started or Learn more, emoji icons, fade-up-everything motion.

contract, re-running concept — as its first fix. That is the documented route for those dispositions
(`skills/jury/SKILL.md`: `rebuild` goes back to `awards:concept`), not a refusal to give fixes.

FAIL if the reply praises the page, gives scores without reasons, or gives no located fixes at all.
