# Kiln Nine

Synthetic demonstration material: Kiln Nine, its collection, specifications and studio are fictional. The product imagery is generated, not a record of manufactured goods. This is a demonstration website; enquiries are not sent to a real studio.

## Collection 01 — Small rituals, held in clay

Three stoneware forms for the table, the shelf, and the pause between tasks. Each shape begins with an ordinary gesture: setting down fruit, gathering a few stems, taking a quiet drink. The collection keeps the hand of its maker visible through a soft lip, a turning line, and a glaze that settles differently at every edge.

The two chalk pieces share a pale, iron-flecked surface. The small cup carries an olive glaze. Warm, unglazed feet reveal the clay beneath each finish.

## The pieces

### 01 — Low bowl

- Dimensions: 24 cm diameter × 7 cm high.
- Material: hand-thrown stoneware; pale chalk glaze with iron flecks; unglazed foot.
- Intended use: serving fruit, bread, or shared dishes.
- Description: A low open form with enough depth to gather, and enough space to see what it holds.

### 02 — Stem vase

- Dimensions: 10 cm diameter × 26 cm high.
- Material: hand-thrown stoneware; pale chalk glaze with iron flecks; glazed interior and unglazed foot.
- Intended use: cut stems or a branch arrangement.
- Description: A straight, quiet silhouette, weighted at the base and open to whatever the season brings.

### 03 — Palm cup

- Dimensions: 9 cm diameter × 8 cm high.
- Material: hand-thrown stoneware; muted olive glaze; unglazed foot; no handle.
- Intended use: cool or warm drinks.
- Description: Rounded to rest in the palm, with a small turning line where the glaze meets raw clay.

## Material and making

The fictional collection is wheel-thrown in small batches and finished by hand. Minor changes in profile and glaze are part of the design. Dimensions describe the intended forms, with an illustrative tolerance of ±0.5 cm. No production certification or food-safety testing is claimed for these synthetic objects.

## Care

Hand-wash with a soft cloth and mild detergent. Dry the unglazed foot before storing. Avoid abrasive cleaners, sudden temperature changes, oven use and direct flame. Do not microwave the vase. Handle hot drinks with care: the cup has no handle.
