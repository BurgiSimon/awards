# Form & Sound

Synthetic demonstration material: Form & Sound, its program, speakers, venue, prices and access arrangements are fictional. This is a demonstration event website. No ticket is sold, no place is reserved, and no personal information is transmitted.

## Three days of making connections

16–18 October 2026 / The Resonance Hall / Bellwick, an invented city

A small gathering for people who shape what we see and hear. Talks become working sessions; sketches become scores; the program ends with a shared listening room. Bring a question, a notebook, and room for something unexpected.
