# Original generated raster assets

Created 2026-09-22 using the built-in image-generation tool, one call per image, four benchmark calls total. These are synthetic demonstration images, not photographs of real products or locations. No external reference artwork was supplied. The second image in each pair used the first generated image as its material, light and palette reference. No additional generation occurred after selection.

Delivery processing: native Chromium canvas re-encoding to WebP at quality 0.88, preserving the full 1536 × 1024 composition without resizing or retouching. Re-encoding omits embedded execution metadata from repository deliverables; original PNGs and raw provenance remain in the external benchmark archive. Each final file is below 1 MiB.

## Asset use

- `assets/coast.webp`: a wide view of the invented slate-and-pebble shore; use to establish the journal's setting.
- `assets/shore-detail.webp`: a closer tidal-pool observation with pebbles, slate and olive seaweed; use alongside the article.
- Both share diffuse overcast light and a sea-grey/olive palette. Preserve a useful near/far relationship in the landscape and the pool's visible water edge in the detail.

## Final generation prompts

### coast.webp

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: original synthetic landscape photograph for a fictional Tidal Notes coastal field journal benchmark.
Shared pair brief: quiet field observations on an invented temperate coastline, dark layered slate, rounded grey pebbles, muted olive seaweed, cool sea-grey water, soft overcast morning light, restrained documentary photography with convincing natural textures. This is an invented location, not an identifiable real coast.
Primary request: wide coastal landscape at low tide with curved pebble shore in foreground, gently layered slate outcrop at one side, calm distant water and a low cloud horizon.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, clear foreground-to-horizon depth, enough open sky and water to breathe, varied small shore details, no dramatic fantasy cliffs.
Constraints: no buildings, boats, people, signage, text, logos or watermark; no stock-photo oversaturation; original synthetic scene.
```

### shore-detail.webp

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: original synthetic shore detail photograph for a fictional Tidal Notes coastal field journal benchmark.
Shared pair brief: quiet field observations on an invented temperate coastline, dark layered slate, rounded grey pebbles, muted olive seaweed, cool sea-grey water, soft overcast morning light, restrained documentary photography with convincing natural textures. This is an invented location, not an identifiable real coast.
Input image: reference image of the coastline; use its stones, slate, seaweed and overcast lighting as material and setting reference.
Primary request: a close field photograph looking down at a shallow tidal pool among rounded grey pebbles and a small layered slate edge, with a few strands of muted olive seaweed resting in clear still water.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, intimate close observation, clearly visible wet stone textures and water edge, asymmetric natural arrangement, no horizon.
Constraints: maintain the reference's restrained sea-grey and olive palette and diffuse light; no animals, humans, buildings, text, logos or watermark; original synthetic scene.
```
