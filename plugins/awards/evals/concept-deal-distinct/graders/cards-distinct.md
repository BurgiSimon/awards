The reply should present three creative directions ("cards") for a sea-salt works' website.

PASS only if all of these hold:
- There are exactly three directions of comparable depth, each with a thesis, a world (colour, type
  voice, materials named in words), a first viewport and a signature interaction.
- The three differ in kind: each rests on a different world drawn from a different source (for
  example tide tables, a lifeboat station's logbook, a chef's pass, chemistry of crystals) AND a
  different signature interaction. Different copy, palettes or adjectives over the same underlying
  idea do not count as different.
- No two directions share the same signature mechanism (for example two that both "reveal salt
  crystals growing as you scroll", or two that are both "a scrolling tide chart").

FAIL if two directions are twins — the same world or the same signature interaction restated — or
if one direction is a thin variant of another, or if fewer than three directions are given.
