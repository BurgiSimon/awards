We design and build websites for cultural and public-sector clients.

Northfield is a four-person studio in Leeds. We have worked with museums, libraries, councils and charities since 2014, from first workshop to launch and support.

Selected work

  • Aire Rivers Trust

    Volunteer sign-up and river monitoring maps, 2023.

Studio

We work in small teams with one designer and one developer on each project. Every site we ship meets WCAG 2.2 AA, and we test it with the people who will use it.

Our services are research, interface design, front-end and back-end development, and support after launch.
