I run Saltglaze, a two-person stoneware studio in a converted boatshed on the Firth of Forth. We fire a wood kiln four times a year, and each firing yields about ninety pieces: mugs, lidded jars, serving bowls and a few large platters. Everything sells out within a week of the kiln opening, mostly to people already on our mailing list.

We need a new website before the spring firing on 14 March. It has to do three things: show the work from the last firing (we have 32 photos, use labelled placeholders for now), explain the firing itself (five days of stoking, ash landing on the pots, why no two pieces match), and collect enquiries for the next opening. No shop or checkout; enquiries go to an email address. Each piece has a name, dimensions in cm, capacity in ml where it holds liquid, a clay body, and care notes (dishwasher safe except the platters).

Audience: collectors and restaurant buyers in the UK and Netherlands, often on a phone. Our current site is a free template and looks like every other maker's. We want something Awwwards-level that still loads fast on a rural connection. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript or Vite is fine; we host on Netlify.

Do not ask me questions; make and record your assumptions.
