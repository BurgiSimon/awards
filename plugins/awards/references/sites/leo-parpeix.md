## 1. Concept and narrative
A first-person craft manifesto, not a case-study index. The site states its own brief in the footer: a balance between an immersive 3D experience and a clean 2D environment, projects readable "in a single scroll", skill set legible "in under 10 seconds" [verified, index.js]. The 3D carries a character — **the moustache daisy**, a bee and a flower "meticulously sculpted in an artist's workshop" [verified, index.js] — which is also the easter egg: the hero invites "(Click to feed the bee)" and spawns 3D fruit with an SFX [verified, index.js `beeMesh`/`flowerMesh`/`_scheduleFruit1Sound`].

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- Smooth scroll: `new Lenis({ lerp: isTouch ? 1 : 0.085, smooth: true, mouseMultiplier: 1, smoothTouch: false, syncTouch: true, infinite: false, autoResize: true, orientation: 'vertical', gestureOrientation: 'vertical' })`, with `ScrollTrigger.update()` on every Lenis scroll event [verified, index.js].
- Easings: the house curve is CSS `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.1,1)` (40 uses); also `(.19,1,.22,1)`, `(.4,0,.2,1)`, `(.45,0,.2,1)`. Two GSAP CustomEases are registered by name: `reveal` = `.4,0,0,1` and `hide` = `.86,0,.07,1`; archive masks use GSAP `expo.out` [verified, index.css + index.js]. *(The card previously recorded `.16,1,.3,1` and `.87,0,.13,1`; both are wrong.)*
- Durations seen: arrow-button reveal 1.125 s on `reveal` with a +0.2 s inner offset; archive title mask ±102 % in 0.35 s `expo.out`; archive media mask `translateY(102%)→0` in 1.3 s `expo.out`; media fade-out 0.55 s on `hide`; line stagger 0.125 s [verified, index.js].
- Framerate-independent damping throughout, as `1 - Math.exp(-k * dt)` with k ∈ {6, 12, 14, 18} [verified, index.js].
- Signature — the fluid wake: a real velocity-field simulation at `resolution: 0.2` of the viewport (a render target, **not** a fixed 128²), options `{ iterations_poisson: 1, iterations_viscous: 0, mouse_force: 80, cursor_size: 40, viscous: 0, isBounce: false, dt: 0.015, deltaFactor: 1.75, dissipation: 0.98, isViscous: false, BFECC: false }`, with BFECC advection available as a switch and the FBO type chosen HalfFloat on iOS, Float elsewhere [verified, index.js]. It feeds one full-frame pass whose defaults are `{ aaType: 'none', fluidHashBlurRadius: 0, fluidDistortionStrength: 0.003, fluidVelocityBlurScale: 15 }` — a velocity-driven **hash blur plus UV distortion**. There is **no chromatic aberration** in the shipped bundle [verified, index.js: 0 occurrences of `chromatic`/`aberration`].
- Quality: `dpr = Math.min(2, devicePixelRatio)` with a separate `postDpr = 2` for the post chain; camera fov 75 [verified, index.js].
- Sound: Howler.js with `Howler.volume(0.7)` as the global default, a named sound registry, per-sound fade durations, and `playSfx` / `pause` helpers; a `PAGE_TRANSITION_SOUND` event exists on the bus [verified, index.js]. *(The card previously said plain `HTMLAudioElement` at .375/.35; that is wrong.)*

## 6. Weaknesses
- **Zoom is blocked twice.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no>` *and* a document `keydown` handler that `preventDefault()`s Ctrl/Cmd + `+ - = 0`, plus `gesturestart` / `gestureend` preventDefault [verified, index.html + index.js]. That is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure; Awwwards still scored Accessibility 7.00.
- **No reduced-motion path.** `prefers-reduced-motion` appears **zero** times in the CSS and zero times in the JS, and `desktop-rm-s00/s100.png` are indistinguishable from the full-motion frames — the hero is still caught mid-mask and the bee still drifts [verified, index.css + index.js + captures].
- **Keyboard: fail.** The only `keydown` listeners in the bundle are the zoom block and Howler's audio unlock. No Escape handler, no `tabindex`, seven `aria-label`s in total; the drag rail and the archive accordion are pointer-only [verified, index.js].
- **Wayfinding.** The scrollbar is hidden globally and no chapter indicator is rendered, so a 20-plus-row archive has no position cue [verified, index.css + captures].
- **Phone.** The layout is genuinely designed (hero bottom-left, two pill controls), but landscape is refused outright by a modal "Rotate your device" [verified, mobile-s00.png + index.js].
- **Responsive by patch.** 44 distinct `max-width` breakpoints at values like 411, 413, 427, 453, 468, 487, 488, 533, 554, 694, 707, 718, 728, 745, 825, 827, 847, 886, 932, 1047, 1054, 1061, 1080, 1119, 1149 and 1241 px — a breakpoint per overflow bug rather than a system [verified, index.css].
- **Load gate.** The navbar reveal is a hard-coded `1s 3.75s` opacity transition, not a real load signal [verified, index.css].
- **Stale data.** The footer object still carries `date: "Spring 2021"` and `copyright: "© 2024"` from the previous portfolio; only the rendered year is computed [verified, index.js + desktop-s100.png].
- What the awards skills do differently: keep the sound-consent gesture and the progress ring but tie the hold to a real load signal; ship three reduced-motion tiers with a static frame for the fluid pass; give the rail and the accordion keyboard paths and the page an Escape route; leave zoom alone; lazy-chunk the GL layer; design a breakpoint set instead of accreting one.

## 7. Principles
1. One global, physically simulated interaction layer beats a dozen isolated hover tricks — and it costs one low-resolution render target, not a fixed grid.
2. Two named eases and one damping constant family give a whole site a single feel; register the curves by name so every component inherits the same vocabulary.
3. Credibility travels as structured metadata — index, discipline, year, team size, agency, award tally — not adjectives.
4. Give the 3D a character with a reason to exist, and the easter egg writes itself.
5. A consent gate is cheap; a content gate is expensive. Ask for the sound, never for the page.
6. Blocking zoom to protect a layout trades a WCAG failure for a bug you could have fixed.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:** the velocity-field post-process as an architecture — a fraction-of-viewport FBO feeding one full-screen distortion pass `[recipe:gl-fluid-wake-post]`; `1 - Math.exp(-k·dt)` damping as the house motion primitive; named CustomEases shared across components; the contextual pointer label driven off an event bus and gated on `pointer: fine` `[recipe:cursor-two-speed]`; a progress ring that resolves into a sound-consent click `[recipe:preloader-counter-hold]`; named theme tokens swapped per route `[recipe:theme-swap-tokens]`; the draggable snapping project rail with a tabular metadata strip `[recipe:horizontal-rail]`; the numbered archive accordion that re-centres the open row; the two-state hero headline `[recipe:split-text-masked-reveal]`; `dpr = min(2, devicePixelRatio)` with a separate post DPR `[recipe:quality-tiers]`; Lenis at lerp .085 driving `ScrollTrigger.update()` `[recipe:boot-lenis-gsap]`.
- **Don't take:** the forest/cream/yellow palette hexes; the "French / Interactive / Designer" hero device; the bee-and-flower scene and the feed-the-bee gag; the section order; the one-chunk 432 KB bundle; the zoom block; the landscape lock; the 44 ad-hoc breakpoints; the absent reduced-motion branch `[recipe:reduced-motion-switch]`.
