## 1. Concept and narrative
"Son Daven is a new place of power in the Carpathians, where Hutsul culture meets contemporary architecture, art, hospitality" [verified fragment, Awwwards entry read 2026-09-18]. The brief is hard: sell an unbuilt building in a war-affected country to investors while seducing future guests. The order is emotion before economics — the scene registry runs prologue and about before factoids, and `initSceneFin` (finance) sits second-to-last, ahead of the footer [verified, slater-55210.js]. The hero states the commercial frame in the eyebrow ("İNVESTMENT PROJECT") and the emotional one in the wordmark, then hands off three plain facts — developer, typology, place — in a single meta row [verified, desktop-s00.png]. Two copy registers, literary in the atmosphere chapters and plainly numeric in the commercial ones ("20+ years of experience", "ranked 2nd among developers in Ukraine by Forbes Ukraine", "37 completed projects") [verified, desktop-s75.png].

## 3. Visual language
Two tokens and three theme classes do all the work: sand ground with near-black ink, the same pair inverted, and near-black with white ink, swapped per section by scroll position [verified, webflow-shared.css + slater-55210.js]. The signature is a **square-grid dither shader**: a texture is sampled on a 100 × 100 cell grid and each cell drawn as a bar whose width runs from `-2%` to `102%` of the cell by luminance, with gamma, black/white point and threshold controls [verified, slater-55210.js]. It is applied to the wordmark, to Hutsul ornament, to landscape photography and to the footer illustration, so renders, folk motif and type all acquire the same woven texture — the identity reads as embroidery rather than as an effect [verified, desktop-s00/50/75/100.png]. Imagery is otherwise responsive WebP at up to 2880 w with full `srcset` ladders [verified, index.html]. The display face renders in the captures as a high-contrast transitional serif at "PROJECT GALLERY" and in the factoid numerals; because the fallback is Times New Roman, the captures alone cannot separate the loaded face from the fallback [verified, mobile-s75.png + webflow-shared.css]. The UI face is a wide monoline squarish grotesque with a dotted capital İ. Spacing ladder `u-0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40, 48, 72, 104, 136, 176` — a 2 px base that widens to 4 px and then to editorial jumps [verified, webflow-shared.css].

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- Eases, registered once as CustomEase: `InOut` 0.76,0,0.24,1 · `Out` 0.25,1,0.5,1 · `In` 0.5,0,0.75,0 · `Ease` 0.25,0.1,0.25,1 · `Write` 0.333,0,0.667,1. Duration tokens `durS 0.4 / durM 0.8 / durL 1.2`, `stagger 0.1`, `delayReveal 0.2`, `breakPoint 992` [verified, slater-55210.js].
- Lenis wiring is the canonical one: `lenis.on("scroll", ScrollTrigger.update)`, `gsap.ticker.add(t => lenis.raf(t * 1000))`, `gsap.ticker.lagSmoothing(0)` [verified, same].
- Text reveal splits by **words**, not lines: `yPercent: gsap.utils.wrap([-150, 75, -75, 150])`, `scale: 0`, `opacity: 0` → rest, `duration: 1.2`, `stagger: { each: 0.025, from: "random" }`, `ease: "Out"` — a scatter-in rather than a mask-up [verified, same].
- Marquee: `repeat: -1`, `xPercent: -100`, `duration: 24`, linear, paused off-screen by a ScrollTrigger, with `timeScale(1 + 0.01 × scroll velocity)` [verified, same].
- Magnetic targets: `[data-magnetic-strength]`, desktop-only behind `ScrollTrigger.matchMedia("(min-width: 992px)")`, release on `elastic.out(1, 0.3)` over 1.6 s [verified, same].
- Theme swap: a ScrollTrigger per `[bg="light|dark|color"]` section whose start/end are the theme element's own vertical mid-point, toggling `.theme_on-*` [verified, same].
- Page transitions: Barba `dither` transition, `sync: false`, scroll position stored per pathname in `sessionStorage` and restored with `lenis.scrollTo(…, { immediate: true })` [verified, same].
- 23 scrubbed ScrollTriggers (18 `scrub: true`, three `scrub: 1`, two `scrub: 0.25`); starts cluster on `top bottom` (18) and `top top` (7) [verified, same].
- Preloader: Flip-based, `Flip.from(…, { duration: 1.8, ease: "InOut" })` over a `scale: 1.5 → 1` on `Out` across 2.4 s [verified, same].

## 6. Weaknesses
- **Reduced motion: fail.** Zero occurrences of `prefers-reduced-motion` in the module or the stylesheet, and `desktop-rm-s00.png` is indistinguishable from `desktop-s00.png` [verified, slater-55210.js, webflow-shared.css, captures]. The page does read at rest — nothing is stranded at opacity 0 — so the failure is a missing branch, not broken content. The awards skills ship three tiers `[recipe:reduced-motion-switch]`.
- **Keyboard: fail on the signature.** No `keydown`/`keyup` listener anywhere in the module; the season handle is pointer- and touch-only [verified, slater-55210.js]. It is saved by the fact that the real `[data-summer-btn]` / `[data-winter-btn]` buttons still exist behind it — a pattern worth keeping. The awards skills make the drag itself arrow-key operable `[recipe:compare-hold-drag]`.
- **Semantics.** 8 `<h1>` elements, one `<nav>`, and **no `<main>`, `<header>` or `<footer>`**; 57 of 70 `<img>` carry `alt=""` [verified, index.html]. Awwwards still scored Semantics/SEO 8.20 and Markup/Meta-data 8.00 — the corpus floor is higher than the award's.
- **Accessibility 7.40** is the entry's lowest developer sub-score and Usability 7.16 its lowest axis overall [verified, Awwwards entry read 2026-09-18]; the comprehension tax of a cinematic loader plus scrubbed chapters plus rails is what the jury docked.
- **Ticker discipline.** The page ticker is clean, but every `[data-lenis-scroll]` rail spawns its own nested Lenis with its own `requestAnimationFrame` loop [verified, slater-55210.js]. The awards skills drive rails from the one shared ticker `[recipe:sticky-stages-rails]`.
- Console noise: CSP `style-src` / `script-src` violations from an embedded Vimeo player, plus 400/401/404s and aborted analytics and Cloudflare-challenge requests [verified, manifest.json]. `pageErrors` is empty — nothing threw.
- The load gate is **already** skippable: repeat visits get the short preloader [verified, slater-55210.js]. Earlier revisions of this card listed that as a fix; it is not one.
- Fonts as .otf instead of woff2 is free weight on the critical path [verified, webflow-shared.css].

## 7. Principles
1. Emotion before economics: earn the spreadsheet by making the place feel inevitable `[pattern:narrative-structures#emotion-before-economics]`.
2. Anchor art direction in a specific culture; specificity is the luxury.
3. One effect, applied everywhere, becomes an identity — a single shader across type, ornament and photography beats six unrelated tricks.
4. Extend provenance to the toolchain (a local foundry for a local story).
5. Solve "not built yet" with time: seasons and a progress log turn renders into evidence.
6. Two tokens and an inverting theme class can carry a whole site; the constraint is what makes it read as designed.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:** the chapter-rhythm long-form with rests between spectacles `[pattern:narrative-structures#chaptered-journey]`; a two-token palette inverted by a theme class swapped on scroll `[recipe:theme-swap-tokens]`; the artboard-locked type scale (`calc(<px>rem / ratio)`, one ratio per breakpoint) `[pattern:typography]`; a named ease set registered once, with three duration tokens; a velocity-scaled marquee that pauses off-screen `[recipe:marquee-raf-mask]`; the sessionStorage two-path preloader `[recipe:preloader-counter-hold]`; keeping real buttons behind an expressive drag control; the two-register copy system `[pattern:copy-and-content#two-registers]`; progress-as-content; interactive location cards over a map; the local-foundry rule.
- **Don't take:** #A89474/#2C2824, KTF Metro, the dither shader's exact parameters, the Hutsul framing, the sheep, the seasonal renders, the chapter order as a template, and the pointer-only season handle.
