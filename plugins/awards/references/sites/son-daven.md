## 1. Concept and narrative
"Son Daven is a new place of power in the Carpathians, where Hutsul culture meets contemporary architecture, art, hospitality" [verified fragment, Awwwards entry read 2026-09-18]. The brief is hard: sell an unbuilt building in a war-affected country to investors while seducing future guests. The order is emotion before economics — prologue and about run before factoids, and finance sits second-to-last, ahead of the footer [verified, slater-55210.js]. The hero opens on a full-colour render of the hotel at sunrise with a woven ridge, clouds, hay and sheep laid over it [verified, live desktop-s008.png]. Two copy registers, literary in the atmosphere chapters and plainly numeric in the commercial ones ("20+ years of experience", "37 completed projects") [verified, desktop-s75.png, 2026-09-18].

The material is the idea: every picture on the page is woven. Film of sheep, birds, clouds, a stork, a river, a person and a mountain is shot or graded on black, cut out by its own blacks and redrawn as vertical bars in the two brand tokens, so render, folk motif, type and film share one texture [verified, slater-55210.js + live captures]. The page closes on a totem of the same weave: a figure on a sheep on a ewe and her lamb, stacked in the middle of the footer between the contact columns [verified, live desktop-footer-t0.png, mobile-s100.png].

## 2. Structure and components
- Load gate: a dithered sheep film (`preloader_sheep.mp4`) and "loading the website" copy; **two paths — the full intro on a first visit, a short one on a repeat, gated by `sessionStorage.hasVisited`** [verified, index.html + slater-55210.js]. The full path pauses its timeline until `Promise.all([120 hero frames, every scene's media, document.fonts.ready])` resolves; the percentage counts scenes loaded [verified, slater-55210.js].
- Header: hamburger + MENU, AEROTOUR (an external virtual tour), centred dithered wordmark, UA locale toggle, CONSULTATION pill; hide-on-scroll [verified, live desktop-footer-t0.png + slater-55210.js]. Fullscreen menu, CTA, media and Vimeo modals [verified, slater-55210.js].
- Hero (desktop): a 2D canvas scrubs 120 WebP frames of the render with `snap: 'frame'` over the hero's height (`scrub: .25`), under a dithered "over" scene; the whole hero scales to 0.3 as it leaves; phones get a still image [verified, slater-55210.js `initScrollVideo`].
- Chapters, each with its own dither scene: prologue (two flowers that play once), about (birds and a stork crossing on loops), seasons, benefits intro and outro, finance, development, factoids (river, sheep, a person), FAQ (birds), footer [verified, slater-55210.js scene registry].
- Season control: a handle dragged along an SVG path with "hold and move" (sun ↔ snowflake), clicking the real summer/winter buttons at 0.4/0.5 [verified, slater-55210.js + live desktop-s066.png]; the switch swaps two dithered films by their levels, not by opacity (§4).
- Sound: an equaliser toggle bottom-left in every frame; the loop starts at volume 0 with `preload: 'none'` [verified, slater-55210.js].
- Footer: location, sales departments, three social links, phone and email at display scale as underlined links, the woven totem, a sand utility bar with copyright and privacy [verified, live desktop-footer-t0.png].
- Sub-pages: `/en/news` and `/en/construction-progress` [recalled medium, not fetched]; article, light and dark, and 404 scenes exist [verified, slater-55210.js `initSceneArticleDark/Light`, `initSceneError`].

## 3. Visual language
Two tokens and three theme classes do all the work, swapped per section by scroll position [verified, webflow-shared.css + slater-55210.js]. The signature is the **bar weave**: each scene's footage is sampled on a cell grid (100 × 100 by default, up to 200 × 150) and every cell drawn as one vertical bar whose width runs from −2 % to 102 % of the cell by darkness, after black and white points, gamma and a threshold [verified, slater-55210.js `createCanvasInstance`]. Near-black, and any cell within two texels of near-black, is discarded, so footage shot on black lands as a cut-out [verified, same]. A black point above the white point inverts the tone, and an ink opacity of 0 makes the picture out of the gaps between bars (the footer mountain, the seasons films) [verified, same]. The weave is set beside full-colour photography and renders rather than replacing them: the hero render, the news cards and the galleries stay in colour [verified, live desktop-s008.png, desktop-s083.png]. The display face renders as a high-contrast transitional serif in headings; the UI face is a wide monoline squarish grotesque with a dotted capital İ [verified, live captures]. Spacing ladder `u-0 … 176`, a 2 px base widening to editorial jumps [verified, webflow-shared.css].

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- Eases, registered once as CustomEase: `InOut` 0.76,0,0.24,1 · `Out` 0.25,1,0.5,1 · `In` 0.5,0,0.75,0 · `Ease` 0.25,0.1,0.25,1 · `Write` 0.333,0,0.667,1. Durations `durS 0.4 / durM 0.8 / durL 1.2`, `stagger 0.1`, `breakPoint 992` [verified, slater-55210.js].
- Lenis wiring is the canonical one: `lenis.on("scroll", ScrollTrigger.update)`, `gsap.ticker.add(t => lenis.raf(t * 1000))`, `lagSmoothing(0)` [verified, same].
- Text reveal by words: `yPercent: wrap([-150, 75, -75, 150])`, `scale: 0`, `opacity: 0` → rest, 1.2 s, `stagger: { each: 0.025, from: "random" }` [verified, same].
- Marquee: `xPercent: -100` over 24 s, paused off-screen, `timeScale(1 + 0.01 × velocity)` [verified, same]. Magnetic targets on `elastic.out(1, 0.3)` over 1.6 s, desktop only [verified, same].
- **Scene settings are tween targets**: GSAP tweens each layer's config object (position, size, black and white points) and the next frame reads it. Hero over: ridge levels 25/200 → 45/175 and hay 25/200 → 75/150 scrubbed from `50% bottom` to `bottom top`; clouds drift `x` with the scroll [verified, slater-55210.js `initSceneHeroOver`]. Benefits intro: three layers grow (a hole render to 148 % wide, people to 324 %, birds to 240 %) on `InOut`/`In` as the section leaves, a fly-through made of rectangles [verified, `initSceneBenefitsIntro`].
- Birds cross scenes on loops: `x` from −33.33 % to 100 % in 5 s, `repeatDelay` 5, played only while the scene intersects (`rootMargin 20%`) [verified, `initSceneAbout`, `initSceneDevBg`, `initSceneFaq`].
- Seasons swap by levels: the outgoing film to black/white 254/255, the incoming to its own points (0/255 summer, 25/150 winter), over 1.2 s [verified, `initSceneSeasons`].
- Page transition `dither`: a 20 × 12 grid of cells `scaleX 0 → 1` in 0.4 s `InOut`, staggered 0.03 from the end across the grid, under a black overlay fading in over 1.2 s; 1.2 s holds each side of the swap; scroll saved per path in `sessionStorage` and restored on `popstate` [verified, `animateTransition`, `initPageTransitions`].
- Preloader: Flip of the logo into the hero over 1.8 s `InOut` while the intro film scales 1.5 → 1 over 2.4 s [verified, `animatePreloaederIntro`].

## 5. Tech and pipeline
Webflow shell, all logic in one Slater-hosted module, libraries from jsDelivr at pinned versions except Swiper 11 [verified, index.html]. Fonts ship as **.otf** [verified, webflow-shared.css]. Scene video lives on `assets.sondaven.com/scenes/` as about twenty MP4 clips; three of them exist only as `.hevc.mp4` with `type="video/mp4"` and no H.264 alternative (`birds_03`, `birds_04`, `footer_mountain`) [verified, slater-55210.js]. Meta: OG title, description, image and an MP4 OG video, Twitter card, JSON-LD; **no `theme-color`, no `color-scheme`** [verified, index.html].

### Tech lens: WebGL
- **Program**: one vertex shader (a full-canvas quad) and one fragment shader per canvas; uniforms `u_texture u_resolution u_texSize u_gridSize u_minWidth u_maxWidth u_threshold u_gamma u_blackPoint u_whitePoint u_bgColor u_fillColor u_bgOpacity u_fillOpacity u_bounds` [verified, slater-55210.js `SHADERS`].
- **Per layer**: the quad covers the whole canvas and fragments outside `u_bounds` are discarded, so every layer costs a full canvas of fragments [verified, fragment `discard`].
- **Cell sample**: `cc = (floor(lc / cs) + .5) × cs`; one texture read at the cell centre; per-channel levels `clamp((rgb × 255 − black) / (white − black))`, then the mean of the channels [verified].
- **Key**: the centre and a generated 5 × 5 texel ring (`BLACK_PIXEL_RADIUS 2`, 24 extra reads) — any near-black or transparent read discards the fragment; the ring is unrolled into the shader source as text [verified, `generateRadiusLoop`].
- **Bar**: `lw = ((1 − br) × (max − min) + min) / boundsWidth × gridX`; fill where `|cell.x − .5| < lw / 2`, else the ground; a hard edge with no anti-aliasing [verified].
- **Context**: `webgl`, `alpha: true`, `antialias: false`, `premultipliedAlpha: false`, `preserveDrawingBuffer: false`, `powerPreference: high-performance`; DPR capped at 1.5 through a `ResizeObserver` [verified, `createCanvasInstance`].
- **Media**: a `<video>` per layer per scene (muted, inline, `preload: auto`, `crossOrigin: anonymous`), ready on `loadeddata`/`canplay` or after 5 s; a `.webm` source gets an `.mp4` sibling first [verified, `loadVideo`].
- **Uploads**: `texSubImage2D` from the video only when `currentTime` changed; a 16-float cache skips repeated uniform calls [verified, `updateVideoTexture`, `uLast`].
- **Loop**: one `requestAnimationFrame` per canvas, capped by `FPS_INTERVAL` (60 by default), started and stopped by an `IntersectionObserver` at `threshold .01`, `rootMargin 20%`, which also plays and pauses the scene's videos [verified, `handleVisibility`].
- **Scenes**: a manager registers each `[data-*-scene]` canvas, collects their `loaded` promises for the preloader's percentage and destroys them on every Barba leave [verified, `initSceneManager`].
- **Footer scene**: two layers on one 1440 × 900 canvas (at DPR 1): a mountain clip across the bottom 32.5 % with levels 240/50 and ink opacity 0 (the mountain is the gaps), and the totem clip at 30 % / 5 %, 40 % × 95 %, a 150 × 125 grid, levels 0/215 [verified, `initSceneFooter` + live report].
- **Count**: fifteen dither canvases on the home page and about 23 M backing pixels between them at DPR 1 [verified, live report.json, 2026-10-28].

## 6. Weaknesses
- **Load gate without a ceiling: fail.** The first-visit intro waits for 120 hero frames, every scene's media and the fonts with `Promise.all` and no timeout; on 2026-10-28, from this container, 17 of the 120 frames were still pending after 40 s and the preloader never lifted [verified, slater-55210.js + live diagnosis]. Videos give up after 5 s; images and frames have no limit [verified, source].
- **Reduced motion: fail.** Zero occurrences of `prefers-reduced-motion` in the module or the stylesheet [verified, slater-55210.js, webflow-shared.css, 2026-10-28]; the dither loops, the bird crossings and the scrubbed levels all run.
- **No pause for looping footage**: the woven films play for as long as their scene is on screen; the only controls are the sound toggle and the season handle [verified, slater-55210.js].
- **Keyboard: fail on the signature.** No `keydown` listener outside a commented-out block; the season handle is pointer- and touch-only, saved by the real summer/winter buttons behind it [verified, slater-55210.js].
- **Fifteen contexts**: one WebGL context per scene sits at the edge of Chromium's sixteen live contexts; a bird clip shown by three scenes is three `<video>` elements and three uploads [verified, source + live page].
- **HEVC only for three clips**: browsers without HEVC decoding never draw the footer mountain or two bird layers; in this headless Chromium those requests aborted [verified, source + failed requests].
- **Semantics.** 8 `<h1>`, no `<main>`, `<header>` or `<footer>` [verified, live report 2026-10-28]; Awwwards still scored Semantics/SEO 8.20.
- Accessibility 7.40 is the entry's lowest developer sub-score and Usability 7.16 its lowest axis [verified, Awwwards entry read 2026-09-18].
- Ticker discipline: every rail spawns its own Lenis and `requestAnimationFrame`, and every scene its own loop [verified, slater-55210.js].
- Fonts as .otf, and a frame decoder (`ActiveFrame.js`) shipped without being used [verified, webflow-shared.css + index.html].

**What the awards skills do differently**: the weave runs from one context with pooled clips and quads sized to each layer [recipe:gl-dither-scene-pool] [recipe:gl-video-dither-layers]; the gate races its loads against a ceiling [recipe:dither-cell-transition]; reduced motion gets one developed still and looping footage a pause button; the season drag is arrow-key operable [recipe:compare-hold-drag]; rails run on the one shared ticker [recipe:sticky-stages-rails].

## 7. Principles
1. Emotion before economics: earn the spreadsheet by making the place feel inevitable `[pattern:narrative-structures#emotion-before-economics]`.
2. Anchor art direction in a specific culture; specificity is the luxury.
3. One effect, applied to every picture, becomes a material — a single treatment across type, ornament, render and film beats six unrelated tricks.
4. Make the treatment's settings the animation: when a picture's levels, size and position are the tween targets, developing, swapping and flying through are the same move.
5. Solve "not built yet" with time: seasons and a progress log turn renders into evidence.
6. Two tokens and an inverting theme class can carry a whole site; the constraint is what makes it read as designed.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:** the weave as one material for every picture, from footage graded on black `[recipe:gl-video-dither-layers]`; one context and pooled clips for many scenes `[recipe:gl-dither-scene-pool]`; levels as state (develop on scroll, swap seasons by black and white points); a transition in the same weave `[recipe:dither-cell-transition]`; woven pictures set beside full-colour ones, not instead of them; the chapter-rhythm long-form with rests `[pattern:narrative-structures#chaptered-journey]`; a two-token palette inverted on scroll `[recipe:theme-swap-tokens]`; the artboard-locked type scale `[pattern:typography]`; a named ease set with three duration tokens; a velocity-scaled marquee that pauses off-screen `[recipe:marquee-raf-mask]`; the two-path preloader `[recipe:preloader-counter-hold]`; real buttons behind an expressive drag; the two-register copy `[pattern:copy-and-content#two-registers]`; a footer that closes the world with its own picture.
- **Don't take:** #A89474/#2C2824 [verified, as recorded under Palette], KTF Metro, the weave's exact grids and levels, the Hutsul framing, the sheep and the totem, the seasonal renders, the chapter order as a template, the pointer-only season handle, and a gate with no ceiling.
