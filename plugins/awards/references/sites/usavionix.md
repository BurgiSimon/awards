## 1. Concept and narrative
The visitor is cast as an operator. The first viewport is an aerial terrain plate under a three-line display headline, "Securing the skies with autonomous intelligence", with the positioning line and a "Scroll To Explore" pill parked bottom-right [verified, `desktop-rm-s00.png`]. Scroll then runs a single live mission as a pinned sequence: boot readouts, a Delta flying a search pattern, an anomaly detection, a confirmation pass, then a tactical map that resolves the incident [verified, `desktop-s25/s50.png`, `desktop-rm-s75.png`]. Only after the mission does the page argue in words: "One platform, many missions." over three named scenarios — **Wildfire Cascade**, **Border Storm**, **Critical Infrastructure in Crisis** [verified, index.html SSR text]. Copy is verb-chain and agent-framed: the product is "The first agent in the air" and the wildfire beat compresses a timeline into one breath — "detect ignition points, suppress advancing fires, secure nearby neighborhoods, and keep evacuation routes open" [verified fragments ≤ 25 words, index.html]. Credibility is a single sentence: "Founded by leaders from SpaceX, Apple, Tesla, Palantir, JPL, and elite military units" [verified, same]. The ask is scarcity-framed — "Request Access", demo slots limited [verified, same].

## 6. Weaknesses
- **Reduced motion — fail, on the evidence available.** `prefers-reduced-motion` appears **zero** times in the stylesheet and zero times in the five largest JS chunks [verified, negative]; the `desktop-rm` run reports `reducedMotion:true` yet still builds 3 WebGL canvases [verified, `manifest.json`]. The consolation: all five `desktop-rm` frames render their content at rest, so nothing is stranded at opacity 0 [verified, captures]. *The awards skills branch the sequence into full / reduced / static tiers and let the reduced tier land on the end state of each beat instead of scrubbing it.*
- **No `h1` on the home page.** The only `h1` in the served document belongs to the 404 boundary; the home page's headings are two `h2`s, and the rest of the copy is client-rendered [verified, index.html + RSC payload]. The entry scored Semantics/SEO 8.00 anyway. *The awards skills put one `h1` in the first viewport and keep the chapter headings in the markup, not in a canvas.*
- **Keyboard — [unknown].** The dialogs are Radix, so the menu, the contact modal and the policy sheets inherit focus trapping and Escape for free [verified, `radix-*` ids]; whether the pinned sequence can be advanced or skipped from the keyboard was not observed.
- **Mobile hero contrast — fail in the captured frame.** In `mobile-s00.png` the headline sits over the terrain plate in a dark red at very low contrast and is barely legible [verified, `mobile-s00.png`]; it may be a transitional state of a scroll-driven reveal, but it is the state a phone visitor can land on. *The awards skills put a scrim or a solid plate under any display type that crosses photography, and test the worst frame, not the best.*
- **The phone is otherwise designed**, not shrunk: `mobile-s100.png` has its own footer stack — chevron mark, full-width outline wordmark, the drone render, a full-width CTA pill, then the legal row [verified].
- **Content defect**: one HUD line reads `alert: POTENTIAL THREATs` — lower-case where every sibling is upper-case, and a stray plural [verified, index.html].
- **Load gate**: it announces politely, which is more than most diegetic preloaders do, but the hold duration and any repeat-visit skip were not observed [unknown]. *The awards skills tie the gate to a real load signal, cap it at ≈ 2.5 s on repeat visits and never gate content behind it.*

## 7. Principles
1. A load gate can carry the concept — and it costs one `role="status" aria-live="polite"` to make that concept accessible.
2. Address the visitor as the operator; build the first viewport as their console.
3. Restraint is not the absence of colour but its rationing: three accents, each meaning exactly one system state.
4. Run one concrete mission end to end before making any abstract claim; the argument reads as evidence.
5. Two type registers — a grotesque for human sentences, a mono for machine output — do the work a five-face system pretends to.
6. Give a pinned sequence a chapter indicator, or the visitor cannot tell progress from suspension.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:** the diegetic load gate with a polite live region `[recipe:preloader-counter-hold]` `[pattern:preloaders-and-transitions]`; role-casting the first viewport `[pattern:hero-archetypes]`; a three-token status palette carried in CSS custom properties `[pattern:color-and-material]`; the sans + mono two-register contract `[pattern:typography]`; the named z-index ladder as a layout contract; verb-chain and "agent" capability copy, and the scenario triptych after the demonstration `[pattern:copy-and-content]`; a chapter indicator over any pinned sequence `[pattern:components-catalog]`.
- **Don't take:** the near-black `#0f0f0f` + cyan/amber/red HUD set as a default palette (it is diegetic here); the Wildfire/Border/Infrastructure triptych and its copy; the thermal/lidar/IR readout lines; the Delta and Phalanx names and renders; Geist + Geist Mono as a reflex pairing; and the one real mistake — shipping a scrubbed cinematic sequence with no `prefers-reduced-motion` branch.
