## 1. Concept and narrative
- **The one idea:** sell outdoor furniture by showing the outdoors first. The hero is not a product shot but a loose field of cut-out products drifting over warm ivory, and the long middle of the page is site photography — moorland, a boardwalk, a golf clubhouse, a planted path — with the objects almost incidental in it [verified, desktop-s00/25/50.png].
- **Beats:** floating product field under the `<h1>` → a 900vh stage of numbered chapters, each captioned *Made to Last* with a running counter (02, 04 seen) and a persistent `Scroll to Explore ↓` → product close-ups with a Colors swatch row (four dots plus "+4") → the collections index as display-scale headings with live counts, a product image tucked inside the heading line between word and count → the yellow footer [verified, desktop-s25/50/75/100.png].
- **Copy register:** functional, near-catalogue. The h1 is the only slogan — "Spaces for people, made for life." — and the one rhetorical line is a pull quote: "We think outdoor furniture should look and feel good while doing good for our planet" [verified fragments, index.html]. Section headings are plain capability statements ("Signage and equipment for all facets of the great outdoors") [verified, same].
- **Contextual conversion:** the chapter stage carries a download card that changes with the chapter — *Golf Catalogue / Download Now ⬇* over the clubhouse beat [verified, desktop-s50.png].

Modal and dropdown inners opt out with `data-lenis-prevent` [verified, index.html].
- **Easing vocabulary**, counted across the three chunks read [verified]: `power2.out` ×24, `expo.out` ×14, `expo.inOut` ×14, `circ.out` ×8, `power2.in` ×6, and two `ease: none`. - **Durations:** the mass sits at .2 (×10), .3 (×8), .4 (×8), .6 (×9) for feedback; 1 and 1.3 for set-pieces; CSS `--duration` tokens .28s / .5s / .75s / .8s [verified, same files]. There is no 1.5s house unit.
- **The long stage:** 900vh of scroll, **no `pin`**, one `scrub: true`, one explicit `ScrollTrigger.create({ trigger, onRefreshInit, start: () => \`top+=${…}px …\` })`, and `gsap.quickSetter` writes for the per-frame work [verified, *.js]. The stage is composited with `position: fixed`, not with pin spacers.
- **WebGL:** three canvases, present under reduced motion too; the entry's own highlights name a scroll-scrubbed zooming video and a 3D material study for the sustainability chapter [verified, manifest.json + Awwwards entry read 2026-09-18]. The mesh layer degrades to plain images by design [verified, DKtYPyz8.js].
- **Reduced motion:** honoured **in CSS** in at least three places — the wishlist timer fill (`animation: none; transform: scaleX(1)`), the chapter stage's scroll label (`animation: none`) and the collections hover media (`transition: none`) [verified, groq.CepGutwA.css + index.html]. `prefers-reduced-motion` appears **nowhere in the three JS chunks read** [verified absence, those files], so the scrubbed stage and the canvases are unlikely to branch. ## 6. Weaknesses
- **The consent wall owns the screen.** Cookiebot covers the centre of all five desktop frames and **the entire 390×844 phone viewport in all five mobile frames**; the phone layout could not be observed at all this pass [verified, all 15 captures]. Usability 7.38 is the entry's lowest axis. **Phone layout: [unknown].**
- **The chrome is not in the document.** The site header and its four primary links render client-side only, and there is **no `<nav>` element anywhere** in the served HTML; `<body>` opens on a `<header>` that is the hero [verified, index.html]. Accessibility 7.00 is the lowest of the six developer criteria.
- **Heading order:** h1 → five h4s inside the chapter stage → h2 and h3 further down [verified, index.html].
- **Shipped markup slop:** a bare `to` attribute on buttons (a Vue prop leaking to the DOM), inline `onerror` handlers on every image, generic `aria-label="Button"` / `"Link"`, and a loading element whose headline is the unresolved CMS fallback "Floema — Floema default site description" [verified, index.html].
- **Shipped console noise, including payloads:** `⛔️ USE TRANSITION ScrollTrigger refresh/update force`, `Contact form payload:` and `[FileRequestForm] API request payload:` all survive into production [verified, CegNqfpt.js, DKtYPyz8.js, DOn5zXw2.js]. Logging a contact form's payload is a privacy defect, not a style one.
- **q=100 on 192 images** is the clearest WPO 7.60 explanation available from the source [verified, index.html].
- **Capture exit 2** with `pageErrors: []`; the only failed requests were six blocked Google Analytics beacons [verified, manifest.json].
- **What the awards skills do differently:** `/awards:structure` server-renders the chrome and gives it a real `<nav>` with a skip link, and keeps heading order monotonic; `/awards:ship` fails a build on a console log that prints form data, sets image quality near 80, ships woff2 rather than ttf, and requires a consent dialog that leaves the phone viewport usable; `/awards:motion` mirrors every CSS reduced-motion branch in JS so the scrubbed stage and the canvases actually fall back, rather than only the three decorative transitions that do.

## 7. Principles (3–6, generalisable)
1. **Name colour tokens after the material, not the role.** `flourYellow`, `stoneBrown`, `natureKraft`, `replasticVistaBlue` tie the palette to what the company actually makes, so the accents carry the collection rather than decorating it.
2. **A long stage does not need `pin`.** 900vh of scroll, one scrubbed trigger and `position: fixed` reaches the same choreography as a pinned section with none of the pin-spacer reflow, and leaves the scrollbar honest.
3. **Photograph the context, not the object.** For a product whose whole argument is that it belongs in a place, the place is the hero and the object earns its close-up later.
4. **Put the conversion inside the chapter.** A download card that changes with the beat converts better than one footer CTA, and costs one slot in the stage.
5. **One variable family at display scale is enough** when the imagery carries the character — but ship it as woff2.
6. **Degrade the canvas explicitly.** An enhancement layer that logs and skips when WebGL is missing keeps the DOM the source of truth; 57 images with 57 alts survive a dead GPU.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:**
  - Lenis on the GSAP ticker with `lagSmoothing(0)` and `lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update)` — the whole page on one clock, native scroll semantics preserved.
  - The 900vh `position: fixed` stage with a single `scrub: true` and `quickSetter` per-frame writes, instead of a pinned ScrollTrigger.
  - `data-section-intersect` as the theme switch: one attribute per section, two token values live, accents held back for swatches.
  - The viewport-unit normalisation block — `--100vw`, `--lvh/--dvh/--svh`, a measured `--native-scrollbar-width` — and the two-term `clamp()` grid gutters.
  - Material-derived token names and a ramp per ground and ink, so mid-steps exist for hover and borders without inventing hexes.
  - A `rel="alternate" type="text/markdown"` mirror of each page.
  - The explicit no-WebGL degrade log and the DOM-first image layer behind the canvases.
- **Don't take:**
  - The token set as a package — #ebe7df + #241f21 + #e9e778 plus the four collection accents is Floema's identity, and Awwwards has already indexed two of them as the site's colours.
  - Zimula, or "one variable display face and nothing else" as a type contract copied wholesale.
  - `q=100` on every image, a variable `.ttf`, or shipping console logs that print form payloads — these are the site's costs, not its craft.
  - A consent dialog sized like this one; on a phone it is the site.
  - The client-only header. If the chrome is the primary navigation, it belongs in the document.
  - The cut-out-products-drifting-over-ivory hero as a composition; it is the entry's signature frame.
