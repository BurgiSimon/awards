## 1. Concept and narrative
The proposition is *market research as people, not data*. The whole site is carried by one commissioned illustration world — flat vector characters, no photography in the hero, no dashboards, no stock boardroom — and the headline says it outright: "Real human insights" over "One global partner" [verified, index.html + desktop-s00.png]. The strategic problem is the old card's read and it still holds [inferred]: qualitative fieldwork is invisible, so the site sells the feeling of being understood rather than the method.

Homepage beats in served order [verified, index.html spine + the five desktop frames]:
1. **Preloader** — an SVG logo drawn on, then lifted away [verified, Preloader chunk].

## 3. Visual language
- **Grounds**: warm beige `#f5f1e4` is the default page; each chapter is a full-bleed slab of one saturated flat hue with a large bottom radius (`--radius-lg` 35 / 50 px), so the page reads as coloured cards stacked on beige [verified, inline CSS + the five desktop frames].
- **Contrast**: ink is charcoal `#2c2e2a` on every ground including yellow and green — high-chroma grounds carry dark text rather than white, which is what keeps the palette legible [verified, desktop-s50/s75/s100.png].
- **Illustration is the identity**: Rive vector characters plus a decorative grammar of hand-drawn marks — blobs, squiggles, dots in the accent hues — scattered as confetti [verified, desktop-s25.png].
- **The wave**: an inline-SVG wavy underline behind accent words and the footer headline, sized by `--wave-h` (9.5 / 20 / 35 / 46 px), `--wave-w` and `--wave-speed: 3s`, painted in the section accent — lilac under "insights", green under "Let's Connect" [verified, inline CSS + desktop-s75/s100.png].
- **Layout**: a 16-column desktop grid dropping to 6 on mobile, `gap-gutter` at `--unit-md: 20px`, content set `col-start-2 / col-span-14` so the outer columns are margin [verified, index.html + inline CSS]. Breakpoints 400 / 500 / 700 / 1000 / 1200 / 1400 / 1600 / 1800 / 2000 / 2400 px. Fluid spacing ladder `--spacing-fluid-xs…-4xl` (`clamp(8rem, 7.3043rem + 3.4783vw, 10rem)` at the top).
- **Radius**: sm 10 / md 15–20 / lg 35–50 px; buttons are pills with a coloured circular icon well (`--button-icon-width` 40 or 80 px) that slides on hover (`--button-label-transform`) [verified, inline CSS + desktop-s50.png].

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- **Preloader** [verified, Preloader chunk, exact]: one GSAP timeline. Wrap `opacity 1`; the logo SVG `y 15% → 0%, opacity 0 → 1, power2.inOut, 1s`; the main path `drawSVG 0% → 100%, power1.inOut, 1s` from the same label; two shadow paths fade `ease:none, 0.25s` at `start+=0.39` and `start+=0.58`; a circle `scaleY 0 → 1, elastic.out(1, 0.3), 1s` at `-=0.25`; the SVG exits `y 0% → -15%, power2.inOut, 1s` at `-=0.45`; `is-first-loaded` on `<html>` at 1.25 s. Completion writes `0` into a nanostore that drives `--preloader-delay`, which the first-hit reveals read.
- **The preloader is killed outright on touch or small screens** — `deviceStatus.isTouchOrSmall` kills the timeline and sets the loaded class immediately [verified, same]. No gate on a phone.
- **Scroll system** [verified, index.html + inline CSS]: a declarative attribute vocabulary — `data-scroll`, `data-scroll-repeat`, `data-scroll-offset` (`20%`, `0%, 40%`, `0,100%`, `-27%,100%`), `data-scroll-position="start, start"`, `data-scroll-speed` from `-0.15` to `+0.15`, `data-scroll-css-progress`, `data-scroll-event-progress="pathProgress"`. Progress lands in `--progress`; CSS derives `--t: clamp(0,1,(--progress − --progress-start)/(--progress-end − --progress-start))` and then `--tx`, `--ty`, `--scale`, `--rot` by interpolating start/end pairs (`--ty-start: 20% → 0`, `--scale-start: .9 → 1`, `--rot-start: ±10deg → 0`). **The tween runs in CSS; JS only publishes the number.**
- **The signature — a stroke used as a wipe** [verified, index.html + desktop-s25.png]: the timeline path is an ordinary `<path fill="none" stroke="#8ED462" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-width="500">` drawn by DrawSVGPlugin against `pathProgress`. At 500 px (250 on mobile) the "line" is a green river flooding the beige ground rather than a route line, and the round cap gives it organic ends. Two hand-authored viewBoxes, `max-md` and `md`, not one scaled path.
- **Sticky stat stack** [verified, inline CSS + desktop-s75.png]: `--stacking-gap` `10vh` or `--spacing-fluid-lg`, `--scale-min: .8`; a sticky left column of copy against the stack.
- **Rails**: rAF marquees at velocity 0.35 / 0.35 / 0.6, two directions [verified, index.html].
- **Parallax**: `c-inner-parallax data-speed="0.1"` on the two image blocks, `will-change: transform` [verified, index.html].
- **Reveals**: `data-reveal`, `data-reveal-delay="0.15"`, `data-reveal-translate-x`, `data-reveal-delay-with-preloader` (true only for the hero Rive), plus `sm:anim-first-hit -delay-1` classes chained off `--first-hit-delay` [verified, index.html].
- **Easing tokens** [verified, inline CSS]: `--ease cubic-bezier(.5,0,0,1)` (the house curve, a sharp expo-out), `--ease-smooth cubic-bezier(.38,.005,.215,1)`, `--ease-bounce cubic-bezier(.17,.67,.3,1.33)`, `--ease-inOut cubic-bezier(.455,.03,.515,.955)`. Durations `.2 / .4 / .6 / .8 / 1s`, default `.4s`.
- **Page transitions**: swup with the Parallel plugin, container `#swup.transition-slide` — outgoing and incoming pages animate simultaneously [verified, index.html + Layout script 1]. Lenis, GSAP and Rive init options were outside the six-file budget [unknown].
- **Rive**: every instance `data-autoplay="true"`; no state-machine name or input in the markup, and `data-disable-firefox-renderer` implies a per-browser renderer switch [verified, index.html]; which Rive runtime and renderer [inferred, medium].

## 5. Tech and pipeline
- Astro islands as **custom elements**, not a framework component tree: every interactive block ships its own `<script type="module">` next to its markup, and each one is a dynamic import with an explicit `__vite__mapDeps` list [verified, index.html + Rail/Rive/HomepageTimeline chunks]. GSAP, DrawSVG, Rive and the scroll engine are all lazy chunks; the eager payload is one 80 KB swup bundle and a 1.3 KB preloader [verified, fetched sizes].
- All CSS is one inlined `<style>` block, ~166 KB before compression, including Lenis's and the consent library's [verified, index.html]. No render-blocking stylesheet request, at the cost of a large HTML document (312 KB).
- Images come from DatoCMS with `?auto=format`, `srcset`, intrinsic `width`/`height`, LQIP as an inline base64 background, and `fetchpriority="high"` on the hero background [verified, index.html]. Fonts are three self-hosted Inter woff2. Only three preconnects/links in `<head>` — no font CDN.
- Measured: 1200 DOM nodes, 14 canvases, CLS 0.0022 desktop / 0 mobile, zero console and page errors; the only failed requests are the analytics beacons, blocked by consent default [verified, manifest.json]. LCP figures in the manifest are a headless SwiftShader cold-cache artefact and are **not** a performance claim about this site.
- Quality tiers: one device switch (`isTouchOrSmall`) that skips the preloader; no DPR or pixel budget, and none needed without 3D [verified, Preloader chunk].

## 6. Weaknesses
The jury's own numbers point at the same place: **Accessibility 6.80 is the lowest of the ten scores** [verified, Awwwards entry], and the source says why.
- **Reduced motion: no CSS branch.** The single `prefers-reduced-motion` query in 166 KB of CSS belongs to the consent library (`#cc-main`) [verified, inline CSS]. `desktop-rm-s00.png` is pixel-identical to `desktop-s00.png` — the hero Rive plays the same under the media query [verified, both frames]. `desktop-rm-s25.png` does show the timeline card fully readable where the motion pass does not, so content is not left hidden [verified, both frames]; whether that is a deliberate JS branch or timing is [unknown], because the reveal engine was outside the six-file budget.
- **`aria-expanded` appears zero times in the document** [verified, index.html]. Desktop dropdown triggers are plain `<a href>` with `data-dropdown-trigger`; the mobile accordions are real `<button>`s with no expanded state. A screen-reader user is never told a submenu exists or opened.
- **Thirteen Rive canvases with no accessible name** — `<c-rive><div><div><canvas></canvas></div></div></c-rive>`, no `role="img"`, no `aria-label`, no fallback text [verified, index.html]. The illustration *is* the brand, and it is invisible to assistive tech.
- **No skip link** [verified, index.html], on a page whose nav carries three mega dropdowns.
- **swup with no a11y plugin** — Head, Preload, Scripts, Parallel and Fragment are registered; nothing announces a route change and no `aria-live` region exists in the bundle read [verified, Layout script 1 + index.html].
- Everything decorative is correctly `aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"`, and the photography carries real descriptive alt text ("Cairo street scene during an ethnographic research study") with 34 empty alts on decorative images [verified, index.html]. The gap is interaction semantics, not content.
- **What the awards skills do differently:** one reduced-motion switch that pauses Rive (`.pause()` on the artboard, not just CSS) and freezes the rails, with a static poster frame behind each canvas; `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls` on every disclosure and `Escape`/arrow keys on the mega menu; `role="img"` and an `aria-label` per Rive instance, or a visually hidden description; a skip link; `@swup/a11y-plugin` or an equivalent live region for route changes; the scroll-progress-to-CSS-variable trick kept, because it is free and it degrades to a static layout when JS fails.

## 7. Principles (3–6, generalisable)
1. **Publish the number, tween in CSS.** Let JS write a single scroll progress value into a custom property and let CSS interpolate transform, scale and rotation off it. The motion survives a failed script as a static layout, and one engine drives every block.
2. **A stroke can be a shape.** A path drawn by scroll at a 500 px stroke width with round caps is a wipe, a fill and a wayfinding line at once — cheaper and more expressive than masking a shape.
3. **Let the illustration be the expression and keep the type neutral.** One workhorse grotesque at three weights, set very large, is enough when the visual voice is carried elsewhere. The restraint is what stops the palette reading as a toy.
4. **Repaint one component set instead of designing per section.** Reassign `--color-primary` and `--accent-color` per chapter; a full-bleed slab with a large bottom radius turns the page into stacked cards for free.
5. **Apply motion to genuinely dry content.** Making a methodology catalogue feel like craft is the hardest and most transferable design problem on this kind of brief.
6. **Kill the load gate where it costs most.** A preloader that is skipped outright on touch and small screens is a decision, not an omission.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:**
  - The declarative scroll vocabulary: `data-scroll`, `data-scroll-offset`, `data-scroll-speed`, `data-scroll-repeat`, `data-scroll-css-progress`, `data-scroll-event-progress` — one engine, zero per-block JS.
  - The `--progress → --t → --tx/--ty/--scale/--rot` interpolation chain in CSS, with per-element start/end pairs.
  - DrawSVG at a stroke width two orders of magnitude above a hairline, with round caps, as a section wipe — and two hand-authored viewBoxes rather than one scaled path.
  - Per-section theme reassignment of two tokens over one warm neutral ground, with dark ink on every saturated hue.
  - The sticky stat stack: `--stacking-gap`, `--scale-min: .8`, sticky copy column opposite.
  - Rails at deliberately unequal velocities (0.35 / 0.35 / 0.6) in alternating directions.
  - A detached CTA pill beside the nav bar, so the primary action is never inside the menu.
  - The preloader shape: draw the mark, punctuate with one `elastic.out(1, 0.3)`, lift it away, and publish completion as a store value other components delay against.
  - Skipping the preloader entirely on touch or small viewports.
  - Islands as custom elements with explicit dependency lists, so GSAP, DrawSVG and Rive are never in the first payload.
  - A methodology × sector × strategy slug matrix where every long-tail page reuses the same kit.
- **Don't take:**
  - The green-and-yellow pair, the five-hue theme set or any hex in the table.
  - Inter at 500 as body weight as if it were a design decision you made.
  - The commissioned character illustrations, the crowd hero, or "13 Rive files" as a target.
  - The hand-drawn wave underline — it is this brand's signature mark, and it is instantly recognisable.
  - The homepage section order hero → timeline → callout → parallax → numbers → articles → rails → footer as a sequence.
  - The four promise lines, the headline, the office block or the *Get a quote* label.
  - The accessibility model: hover-only mega menus on `<a>` elements, `aria-expanded` nowhere, unnamed canvases, no skip link.
