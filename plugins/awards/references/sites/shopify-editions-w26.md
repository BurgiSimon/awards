## 1. Concept and narrative
- **The one idea:** merchants as Renaissance masters equipped with AI. Awwwards' own line: "150+ updates to Shopify, set in a mashup of generative paintings with elements of modern commerce" [verified, Awwwards entry, 18 words].
- **The hero states it in one image:** two figures in classical dress leap toward each other, index fingers almost touching in a Creation-of-Adam quote, one carrying a hot-pink coffee cup, the other hot-pink shopping bags, a graffitied skateboard beneath. The release title and tagline sit in the gap between the hands [verified, desktop-s00]. Tagline: "A new world of commerce. 150+ product updates." [verified, index.html + desktop-s00, 8 words].
- **The beat repeats twelve times:** painted scene → torn-paper reveal of the paper layer → giant grotesque chapter word → serif statement with a script drop cap → a two-column grid of update title + summary + "Read help doc ↗" [verified, desktop-s25/s50/s75/s100].
- **Modern objects are collaged into each painting:** a monitor running code beside an easel, a roadside billboard playing a skate clip with blackletter graffiti type, ragged cut-out birds [verified, desktop-s25, desktop-s50].
- **Register gap is the charm:** art-historical visuals, flat release-note prose. The same structural trick as [site:oryzo], inverted.
- **The series through-line is "a changelog deserves a concept"** — each Edition invents a world inside the same container [recalled high].

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- **Authored motion tokens** [verified, `asset-tailwind-G-N6aznT.css`]: `--fade-out-duration .52s` / `cubic-bezier(.25,.46,.45,.94)` · `--slide-duration .6s` and `--lines-complete-duration 1s`, both `cubic-bezier(.72,.16,.19,.96)` · `--scroll-state-duration .6s` / `cubic-bezier(.41,.19,.13,.95)` · `--lines-duration 3s` / `cubic-bezier(.34,.22,.47,.84)` · `--lines-loading-duration 6s` / `cubic-bezier(.45,.28,.34,.84)` · `--hover-interaction-duration .3s` · `--fade-initial-delay .2s` · `--stagger-fade-in-delay 75ms` · `--stagger-slide-delay 20ms`. **None of these is an expo-out**; the vocabulary is five bespoke cubic-beziers, all slow-in / hard-out.
- **Named keyframes** [verified, same CSS]: `load-lines`, `line-fade`, `nav-item-in`, `stagger-fade-in`, `media-entrance`, `orbit-path`, `timeline-start`, `search-loading-rotate`/`-stroke`, `show-close-button`, `emoji-rise`.
- **Chapter transition — corrected.** It is a **torn / deckled paper edge**, not a brushstroke: the cream layer rises over the painting behind a ragged tear with fibrous tendrils [verified, desktop-s25 and desktop-rm-s25]. The pattern files citing this site for "brushstroke-edged chapter wipes" are **contradicted**; the transferable idea survives as *a torn material edge*, and the earlier claim's source was an award-citation paraphrase, not the page.
- **Depth technique — corrected.** The scenes are **real geometry**: 16 `.glb` models, one per chapter plus props (`EW26_Hero_…`, `EW26_Sidekick_…`, `POS_V53_environment_…`, `Rigged_Book_CS_Animated_V5_…`, `key.glb`), authored in Blender, textured with KTX2 [verified, manifest.json + Awwwards entry]. No `depthMap`, `occlusion` or parallax-occlusion signature anywhere in the served text [verified — negative]; the prior "image + depth map, no geometry" reconstruction is retired. `parallax` survives only as a small DOM component (`cloud-card-parallax`) [verified].
- **Theatre.js drives the scenes**, one project-state JSON per scene, which is what "designers direct the motion" means concretely [verified].
- **Reduced motion is designed, not bolted on.** Nine `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks [verified, CSS]: `::view-transition-old/new/group(*) { animation: none !important }`, `.orbital-ring { animation: none; offset-distance: 0 }` with its tooltip forced visible, `.merch-modal { transition: none }`, plus `motion-safe:` / `motion-reduce:` variants on the nav entrance. `desktop-rm-s00` is **visually identical** to `desktop-s00` — nothing stranded at opacity 0 — and `desktop-rm-s25` stills the in-scene video (the monitor goes blank) while keeping the painting and the torn edge [verified].

## 5. Tech and pipeline
- **Three fallback rungs, verified by filename** [verified, index.html]: full scene (`.glb` + `<Chapter>_desktop.ktx2`) → a per-viewport still (`Fallback_Mobile_<Chapter>_2x.ktx2` with a paired `.jpg`, and multi-frame `Fallback-Retail-Frame1/2/3-Desktop|Mobile.webp`) → the paper-and-ink card layer, which is plain semantic DOM. Every update's Rive file has the same two-rung still fallback. This is the strongest verified instance of "design the degradation" in the corpus.
- **Entry payload is light for the ambition:** twelve JS chunks preloaded, of which the two largest fetched are 252 KB and 243 KB uncompressed; one 130 KB Tailwind stylesheet and a 1 KB font stylesheet; **no GL library in the initial document** [verified, fetched sizes]. 4,182 DOM nodes desktop [verified, manifest.json].
- **Media discipline:** 345 `<img>`, **345 with an `alt` attribute**, 344 `loading="lazy"`; `<video>` elements carry `data-src`, `muted`, `playsinline`, `loop` and are not eagerly loaded [verified, index.html].
- **Capture-run errors** [verified, manifest.json]: `pageErrors` **empty**; `consoleErrors` only `"Animation with name autoplay not found."` twice, mobile only — a Rive state-machine name lookup failing, matching the Rive-per-update system; `failedRequests` are 16 `.glb` aborts on desktop and 12 on desktop-rm, i.e. the harness tore down before the models finished, which is also why the manifest's LCP figures are a headless SwiftShader cold-cache artefact and are **not** quoted here as a performance claim.
- **Resize / quality tiering:** the desktop-vs-mobile KTX2 pairs prove a viewport tier [verified]; a measured-FPS tier [unknown this pass, recalled medium-low from a third-party analysis].
- **SIBLING — Spring '26 "Everywhere"** [recalled high for Spring '26 — Codrops via search]: one fullscreen canvas, scene-window mounting with disposal, scroll → ref → uniforms rather than React state, KTX2 array textures for volumetric light, stacked RGB+alpha video, a custom quantized point-cloud format, a four-tier GPU system. Indicative of the family, not evidence for this page.

## 6. Weaknesses
- **The jury's own soft spots are now known:** Usability 7.51 and Accessibility **7.00** are the two lowest numbers on an 8.05 Dev Award [verified, Awwwards entry] — the craft ceiling here is interaction quality, not visuals (Animations/Transitions 9.40).
- **Focus ring is 1 px with a −1 px offset in #739bff on #f7f7ee** [verified, CSS] — thin, and inset rather than outset. A visible-focus finding on any craft-floor audit, and a plausible part of the 7.00.
- **No `<footer>`** and no designed close: the page ends on the last Developer update list, with only rail-foot legal links [verified, desktop-s100 + index.html].
- **The `<h1>` is the header lockup**, not the release title, which is an `<svg>` elsewhere [verified, index.html] — consistent with Semantics/SEO 7.80.
- **Rail active state can lag the visible chapter** — `desktop-s50` shows "Marketing" at full scale while the rail marks Retail (IV) [verified]; a wayfinding imprecision, possibly mid-transition.
- **No `theme-color` / `color-scheme`**, so the browser chrome is never themed on a site this committed to a world [verified — negative].
- **Asset weight is real**: 16 `.glb` scene models plus 13 desktop KTX2 textures plus 27+ Rive files. The compression and fallback work is what makes it viable [verified].
- **What the awards skills do differently:** keep the three-rung degradation and the per-viewport texture pair as defaults; make the focus ring ≥ 2 px, outset, with a contrast-checked colour; put a designed close and a real `<footer>` at the end; make the `<h1>` the page's subject; keep the rail's active state bound to the same scroll number that drives the scene. Assets with an AI substrate are labelled synthetic in the brief. `bg-black` is allowed as a diegetic stage only when declared in `AWARDS.md ## Exceptions`.

## 7. Principles (3–6, generalisable)
1. **Give a boring content type a concept.** The content did not change; the frame did — 150 release notes became twelve painted rooms.
2. **Spend the third typeface twice.** A face used in exactly two places (a wordmark ligature and a drop cap) reads as a decision; used everywhere it reads as decoration.
3. **Design the degradation, not just the maximum.** Ship a named still for every scene and every animated item, per viewport, before shipping the scene.
4. **Put the motion controls in designers' hands.** A timeline tool with one project-state file per scene removes the engineer from the iteration loop.
5. **Keep the scroll native and the reading layer real DOM.** The canvas is `aria-hidden`; every item is a heading with an id, so search, deep links and find-in-page all still work.
6. **Reinvent the concept every release; keep the container.** Same header, edition switcher, rail, cards and search — a new world each time makes a series, not a redesign treadmill.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:**
  - The three-rung asset ladder: scene (`.glb` + compressed texture) → a named per-viewport still → semantic DOM, with the still authored, not auto-generated.
  - Per-item micro-animation with a paired still (`item_desktop-fallback.png` / `item_mobile-fallback.png`) when a page carries a hundred small demonstrations.
  - Designer-directed motion through a timeline tool, one project-state file per scene.
  - A persistent numbered rail with dotted leaders and an active state, bound to the same scroll number that drives the scene.
  - A motion vocabulary of four or five bespoke cubic-beziers with named duration tokens, rather than one library easing everywhere.
  - Reduced motion that keeps the world and stills the moving parts: disable `::view-transition-*`, freeze orbit paths, force their tooltips visible, blank in-scene video — never hide content.
  - A deep-linkable id on every item, a combobox search, an edition/version switcher, a skip link, `alt` on all 345 images and lazy-loading on all but the first.
  - A treated reveal edge (torn, fibrous, flecked) instead of a hard line, in your own material.
- **Don't take:**
  - The Renaissance / old-masters world, the "Ren*ai*ssance" script-ligature pun, the painting-per-chapter device, or a torn-paper edge as a skin.
  - #f7f7ee / #292919 / #dcdcd0 / #e2e2d9 / #739bff, or HWCigars + NeueMontreal + ImperialScript as a trio [verified, header table].
  - The twelve-chapter product taxonomy, the header/rail composition, or Shopify's artworks, Rive files, videos and launch copy.
  - The 1 px inset focus ring, the missing `<footer>`, or the nav-lockup `<h1>`.
