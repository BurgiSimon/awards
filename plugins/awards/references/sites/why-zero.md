## 1. Concept and narrative
You sign the manifesto before you read it: the load gate asks you to draw a zero, and the closed stroke unlocks the experience. The served stage array runs `gate0to1 → stage1 → gate1to2 → stage2 → stage3 → gate3to4 → stage4 → gate4to5 → stage5` — five stages and four gates, with stage 2 handing off to stage 3 directly on an `advanceAtEnd` threshold rather than through a gate [verified, main.js]. Each stage owns a scroll budget in viewport heights: 175, 250, 325, 368, 50; every gate is a fixed 50 vh that auto-scrolls itself over 3.5 s, 3 s, 3 s and 0 s [verified, main.js]. Asset names carry the argument — `stage2_glass-shatter.glb`, `glass_shards.glb`, a `leather-money-shreds` atlas, a `board-certificates` atlas, `tunnel_new_new.glb`, `origami_certificate.webp`, `garden-godrays.ktx2` — so the beats read as: promise, shattered glass, shredded certificates and burning money, the ZERO tunnel, a world map, and a waitlist folded as origami [verified, main.js]. A persistent XP counter accumulates in the HUD (`.hud-xp`, `.xp-ripple`) [verified, main.css]. Tone: defiant, gamified; the argument is delivered as physics.

## 2. Structure and components
Participatory load gate (`.loader-circle-ring` / `-handle` / `-stage`, a `.loader-degree` readout and a counting `.loader-digit`) [verified, main.css] · four hold-to-proceed gates with an SVG progress ring [verified, main.js] · a **desktop scroll ruler** with per-stage jump buttons (`<button class="scroll-ruler-nav-btn" aria-label="Go to stage N">`, disabled past the frontier) [verified, main.js] · a **separate mobile chapter timeline** (`.mobile-timeline`, `.mtl-bars`, `.mtl-count`, `.mtl-menu`) [verified, main.css] · HUD with menu dropdown, audio toggle, status label, XP and a waitlist button [verified, main.css] · a **WebGL custom cursor** drawn from a 3 × 2 atlas of 56 px cells with six states — arrow, pointer, grab, grabbed, text, move — switched off a list of real interactive selectors including `[role="button"]` and `[role="link"]` [verified, main.js] · interactive world map with an on-screen pad and a virtual joystick · origami waitlist form with a referral link · stage-transition overlay with a spinner. No conventional sections: the layout is a camera path.

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- Virtual scroll: one eased float drives everything; gates hold it with `setHeld`, stages fence it with `_scrollClampMin`, and subscribers receive `progress` [verified, main.js].
- Hold-to-proceed: `holdDuration` seconds tracked on `performance.now()` through `requestAnimationFrame`, an SVG `strokeDashoffset` ring at radius 46, and a **500 ms resume window** that gives back partial charge if you release and press again. The hold sound is rate-scaled by `holdRateScale: 0.75` so its length matches the hold [verified, main.js].
- Adaptive quality, verified in full [main.js]: a 60-frame rolling mean of frame time, downgrade above **22 ms**, upgrade below **12 ms**, **180-frame cooldown** between changes. HIGH = DPR min(dpr, 2), lens blur 24 samples / maxBlur 16, coin ring 24 segments, text RT scale 1. MEDIUM = DPR min(dpr, 1.5), 16 samples, 16 segments. LOW = DPR 1, **lens blur off**, 12 segments, text RT scale 0.75. Frost trail stays half-resolution and foreground noise stays at 0.04 on all three tiers.
- Easing vocabulary is GSAP's: `power2.out` / `power3.in` / `power2.inOut` for transitions, `back.out(2)` for the joystick's return to centre, plus one registered CustomEase [verified, main.js].
- Audio: Howler with 14 tracks — four ambience stems keyed to stages 1, 2–3, 4–5 and 5, plus click, glass-shatter, hand-entry, hold-button, loader, loader-drag, money-burn, tunnel, whoosh and xp-topup, each with its own volume, loop flag and fade in/out [verified, main.js]. A HUD toggle sets `aria-pressed`, and the choice survives a resume [verified, main.js]. Consent handling beyond that toggle [unknown].
- The "hexagonal text blur" this card previously named is **not in the served build**: there is a `lensBlur` post pass with a sample count and a focal radius, and narrative type is a sprite atlas, not shader-rendered glyphs [verified absent, main.js].

## 5. Tech and pipeline
Nine Draco-compressed `.glb` models, nine KTX2/Basis texture **atlases** (including a `clouds-mobile` variant), 14 MP3s and a handful of WebP UI textures, all under hashed Vite paths; Draco and Basis decoders are self-hosted under `/vendor/` [verified, main.js]. Assets are demand-loaded per stage — `assetLoader.loadStageAssets('stage2')` fires from inside stage 1's `enter()`, so the next chapter streams while you read the current one, and each stage releases its atlas on teardown [verified, main.js]. lil-gui is a lazy dynamic import, so the debug panel is not in the entry chunk [verified, main.js]. The served entry bundle is 1.19 MB and the stylesheet 20 KB, both uncompressed [verified, `content-length` headers]. Fonts ship as `.otf`, not WOFF2 [verified, main.css].

## 6. Weaknesses
- **Reduced motion: fail.** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` occurs once in the CSS and once in the JS, and both only flatten the audio-wave indicator in the HUD [verified, main.css, main.js]. The reduced-motion capture is pixel-for-pixel the same experience as the default run [verified, desktop-rm-s00.png vs desktop-s00.png].
- **Gates: fail, and instructively.** The trigger is a real `<button class="next-stage-btn" aria-label="Continue to next stage">`, but every listener — `click` in tap mode, `pointerdown` in hold mode — is bound to `this._hit`, a `<span class="next-stage-hit" aria-hidden="true">` **inside** it, and no `keydown`/`keyup` is ever bound. The button takes focus and announces itself, then does nothing when you press it [verified, main.js].
- **Keyboard elsewhere: pass**, which makes the gate gap look like an oversight rather than a policy. The world map binds WASD to a normalised joystick vector and the arrow keys to the four pad buttons, with an `INPUT`/`TEXTAREA`/`contenteditable` guard, a modifier-key guard and a `blur` reset; menus and modals close on Escape; the waitlist join button handles Enter and Space [verified, main.js].
- **DOM behind the canvas: fail.** Four elements ship in `<body>`; with WebGL off there is a gradient, a decorative "99" and an `aria-label` [verified, index.html]. 397 DOM nodes on desktop and 122 on mobile after boot, all injected [verified, manifest.json].
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled** — `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no` [verified, index.html].
- **The load gate: pass on repeat.** `sessionStorage['zero:resume']` stores the stage, the audio preference and a timestamp, is honoured for 1800 s and deliberately refuses to skip `gate0to1` or `stage1`; a `#stage=<id>` hash deep-links any stage [verified, main.js].
- **The phone: pass.** Type is re-scaled rather than shrunk, the loader counter has its own phone sizing in `svh` units, a mobile-only cloud atlas ships, and eccentric desktop wayfinding is replaced by a dedicated chapter timeline [verified, mobile-s00.png, mobile-s100.png, index.html, main.js].
- **Wayfinding: pass.** Stage-jump buttons on desktop, a chapter timeline on mobile, both labelled [verified, main.js, main.css].

What the awards skills do differently: bind the gate's `keydown`/`keyup` to the button itself, not to a hit-target child, so Enter and Space complete the same hold; ship a real reduced tier that cuts between stages instead of animating them, rather than one rule on a HUD indicator; mirror the narrative type in the DOM behind the canvas; and never disable pinch-zoom.

## 7. Principles
1. Enact the thesis physically — shatter, burn, shred — rather than stating it.
2. A participatory load gate converts loading into commitment, so long as the gesture has a keyboard twin.
3. Progression that unlocks borrows the one thing games have over marketing pages; pair it with a frontier-aware jump control so it never becomes a corridor.
4. Brand geometry can become environment: the logotype extruded into a tunnel cross-section.
5. Owning the scroll is what makes non-linear narrative possible — one eased float, one `held` latch, one clamp.
6. Tie quality to measured frame time with hysteresis and a cooldown, not to a device string.
7. A resume window is cheaper than a skip button and kinder than neither: store the stage, honour it briefly, and refuse to skip the opening.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:** the virtual-scroll float as a single source of truth, with `held` and `clamp` as its only two controls; N stages joined by N−1 gates each costing a small physical commitment; the hold's 500 ms partial-charge resume window; the frame-time quality manager (60-frame mean, 22 ms down / 12 ms up, 180-frame cooldown, DPR → blur samples → LOD → render-target scale); per-stage demand loading with teardown; DRACO + KTX2 atlases + self-hosted decoders as the default pipeline; a cursor whose states are driven off real interactive selectors; a stage-jump ruler on desktop and a chapter timeline on phones; a short-lived `sessionStorage` resume plus a `#stage=` deep link `[recipe:gl-virtual-scroll-camera]` `[recipe:quality-tiers]`.
- **Don't take:** the draw-a-zero gesture, the shattered-diploma metaphor, the ZERO tunnel, the XP counter as-is, the frost-and-mint palette or any of its hexes, the five-stage script, the `aria-hidden` hit-target pattern (it is the bug), `user-scalable=no`, or shipping OTF instead of WOFF2.
