## 1. Concept and narrative
Restraint as luxury. The site earns awe with coordinates and measurements instead of superlatives: dual units everywhere ("3,000 m (10,000 ft)"), scarcity as numbers ("fewer than 500 people each year", "12 guests each"), sentences that close on a turn, and engineered warmth set against a hostile continent. Each camp is positioned as its own character (a former science base turned contemporary lodge; a base "designed as an intergalactic space station"; an adrenaline hub) [verified copy fragments]. Conversion is a conversation: enquire or book a call, never a cart.

## 3. Visual language
Light-dominant, two-token UI, photography carries every ounce of colour and emotion (ice, pods at dusk, Emperor colonies, jets on blue ice) [inferred high from the colour count and category]. Editorial layout alternating full-bleed imagery with generous whitespace; content is the highest-scoring axis (7.74) — depth and editorial quality carried the entry.

## 6. Weaknesses
Usability 7.27 is mid-range; nothing documented about reduced motion. The awards skills treat this card as the proof that content and photography can win, and add what the sources cannot show: reduced-motion tiers, keyboard paths and a designed 404.

## 7. Principles
1. Specificity beats adjectives: numbers, dual units, named coordinates.
2. Lock the UI palette to a ground and an ink; let imagery supply all chroma.
3. Give each property or SKU its own concept sentence and visual character instead of a shared template.
4. A "how this is even possible" chapter de-risks a high-ticket decision.
5. Match the conversion to the price point (enquire / book a call).

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:** specification-grade copy rules (dual units, scarcity as numbers, sentence-ending turns); two-colour discipline with photographic chroma; the named-property pattern; a credibility/operations chapter; a sustainability or ethics section as permission structure; enquiry-led CTA; a headless content model mirroring the narrative spine (Explore / Stay / Learn / About) `[pattern:copy-and-content]` `[pattern:narrative-structures]`.
- **Don't take:** the Antarctic subject, the camp names and their concept sentences, the exact IA labels, any copy line.
