## 1. Concept and narrative
- The one idea [inferred from verified mechanism]: you meet the driver through the visor. The hero is a depth-mapped portrait plane with a wireframe helmet over it running a scanline shader, and a fluid-simulation cursor smears across both [verified, engine-main.js]. The metaphor is executed in GLSL, never captioned.
- The fork [verified routes, inferred meaning]: `/on-track` is the racing self, `/off-track` the civilian self, and the homepage stages the choice as a single pair of headings with a hand-drawn lime scribble marking the active side [verified, desktop-s50.png].
- Beats read off the captures: cream hero over a contour-map ground with a "NEXT RACE / BAKU GP" card pinned bottom-left [verified, desktop-s00.png] → an olive-green archive of captioned press photographs at mixed scales with a serif pull-quote and a lime signature draw-on [verified, desktop-s25.png] → the ON TRACK / OFF TRACK fork [verified, desktop-s50.png] → a near-black interstitial with a lime laurel-helmet mark and one CTA, closing on a curved arc edge into cream [verified, desktop-s75.png] → a store chapter in full-width Mona Sans caps → a dark-olive footer slab with a notched visor-shaped top edge on a cream-to-lime gradient ground, a partner marquee and the full route list [verified, desktop-s100.png].
- Copy register [verified, captures]: short, athletic, first-person. The pull-quote "It doesn't matter where you start, it's how you progress from there." and the footer line "ALWAYS BRINGING THE FIGHT." are the whole voice. Awwwards' own description calls it "capturing his energy and racing passion through dynamic interactions, bold visuals" [verified, Awwwards entry].
- Commercial purpose: partnership inventory, an external store (`store.landonorris.com`), and fan capture via Klaviyo [verified, index.html].

## 2. Structure and components
- Routes [verified, index.html hrefs]: `/`, `/on-track`, `/off-track`, `/calendar`, `/partnerships`, `/legal/privacy-policy`, `/legal/terms-conditions`, plus a 404 template (the `not-found` GL scene).
- Boot / load gate [verified, engine-main.js tail]: `history.scrollRestoration="manual"` → `scrollTo(0,0)` → await the `page-transition` Rive file → `Promise.all([ GL load, a promise resolved by the "allriveloaded" event ])` → init. Real readiness, never a timer. No timeout and no skip control.
- Router [verified]: Taxi fetches the next route with `X-Requested-With: Taxi`, swaps `[data-taxi-view]`, honours `data-taxi-nocache` / `data-taxi-ignore`; GL scenes are destroyed and rebuilt per route.
- Page transition [verified]: `page-transition.riv`, one artboard and one state machine with three boolean inputs — `initial`, `transition-out`, `transition-in` — drawn Cover/Center over the page, hidden again 500 ms after `transition-in`.
- Nav [verified]: `[data-nav-theme]` light/dark sampled from the section currently under the header; a Rive hamburger canvas that gets `aria-label="Open menu"` and `aria-expanded` set in JS; `aria-current="page"` on the active link.
- Rive inventory [verified, 8 files]: `page-transition`, `btn-ui` (the nav hamburger and button icons live here as artboards — the card's earlier `hamburger.riv` was an artboard name, not a file), `phrases`, `signature`, `reef`, `circuits`, `mob-landscape`, `ln4`. Rive artboards can be scroll-scrubbed: `[data-rive-scrolltrigger]` drives a state-machine number input 0→1000 on `scrub: 0.5`.
- GL scenes [verified]: `head` (hero), `tracks` (calendar circuit), `background`, `carousel` (MSDF text top and bottom over a 3D carousel), `helmet-scroll`, `not-found`.
- Architecture [verified]: every scene renders to its own render target, then draws onto a unit plane in one shared scene positioned from DOM bounds plus `window.lenis.scroll` — so DOM and canvas stay locked together without a second scroller. Scenes mount by attribute (`[data-gl]`) and only render while a `[data-gl-track="…"]` ScrollTrigger says they are on screen.
- Marquees [verified]: CSS-driven (`data-css-marquee-list="left|right"`) plus a JS set with `data-marquee-speed` / `-direction` / `-scroll-speed`, duplicated nodes and a velocity-linked direction target.
- Easter eggs [verified]: a `disco` DOM event tweens `uHelmetTransition` 0↔1 over 2 s `expo.inOut`, swaps in disco helmet textures, a matcap, a mask and a lens flare, and adds `gl__is-disco` to `<html>`; `disco-02.glb` is a real model. `?debug` unlocks dat.GUI, stats-gl, OrbitControls, light helpers and extra shadow/HDRI variants. `window.toggleGLColorDebug` and a `console.time("debug")` ship to production.
- No DOM custom cursor [verified, no cursor element or `data-cursor` hook]. The pointer effect is inside the GL layer.

## 3. Visual language
- Grounds [verified, captures]: cream `#efefe5`/`#f4f4ed` for the hero, fork and store; dark olive `#282c20` for the archive and footer; near-black `#111112` for the interstitial; lime `#d2ff00` for buttons, the signature, the laurel mark and the final gradient. Sections are separated by curved and notched edges — arcs, ellipses and a visor-shaped cut-out — not straight rules.
- Type contract [verified]: an editorial display serif (Brier) set against a heavy variable grotesque (Mona Sans Bold), often interleaved inside one headline — "ON" serif / "TRACK" grotesque, "ALWAYS BRINGING THE FIGHT." alternating word by word. Display sizes are hard rem values (`--text--impact: 7.9375rem`, Brier `strong` at `8.25rem`, `line-height: 83%`, `letter-spacing: -0.1875rem`) with per-breakpoint overrides.
- Fluid scale [verified, inline `<style>` in index.html]: `--fluid-container: clamp(var(--min-width), 100vw, var(--max-width))` and `--fluid-font: calc(var(--fluid-container) / var(--design-width) * var(--design-unit) * var(--scale-factor))`, with `--design-width: 1728` and `--design-unit: 16` on desktop, re-declared per breakpoint (768–991 px uses `--design-unit: 20`). One formula, one comp width, every size derived.
- Materials [verified, GL manifest]: PBR sets for the helmet (four diffuse variants — lime, dark, grid, disco — plus normal, roughness, metallic), a glass set, a plastic matcap, a track matcap, a noise texture, and three HDRI variants of `studio_small_08_1k` (light, faded, dark). The hero head is a diffuse + depth + alpha + normal + roughness + shadow stack — a 2.5D depth-parallax portrait, not a mesh.
- Shape language [verified, wf-shared.css + inline styles]: SVG `mask-image` on grid items and the calendar track (helmet-silhouette and circuit-shaped masks), `clip-path: ellipse(...)` hover reveals, and a pale topographic contour field behind the light sections.
- Browser surfaces [verified]: custom `::selection` (lime on black, inverted under `[data-theme="lime"]`), `:where(:focus-visible) { outline: 2px solid var(--color--lime); outline-offset: 2px }`, and `is-safari` / `is-iphone` classes that re-tune footer aspect ratios and marquee widths. No `theme-color` or `color-scheme` meta [verified, index.html].

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- Smooth scroll [verified]: Lenis 1.1.20 on the library defaults — `lerp: 0.1`, `smoothWheel: true`, `autoRaf: false` — driven from the GSAP ticker; programmatic jumps use `duration: 1.2` with an ease-in-out-quad. `data-lenis-prevent` guards inner scrollers.
- Easing vocabulary [verified, counted in engine-main.js]: `power2.out` (36), `none` (21), `power2.inOut` (17), `expo.inOut` (12), `power1.inOut` (11), `power3.out` (10), then `expo.in/out`, `elastic.out(1, 0.75)`, `back.out(1.1)` / `back.out(1.2)`, `power4.inOut`. CSS carries one pair: `--cubic-default: cubic-bezier(0.65, 0.05, 0, 1)` and `--duration-default: 0.75s`.
- Text [verified]: a SplitText wrapper splits every `[split-text]` into `.line` / `.word` / `.char`, with `aria: "auto"`, a `-mask` wrapper per line at `overflow: clip`, a clip-path polygon on `.line`, an explicit screen-reader clone, and a re-split on the `document.fonts` `loadingdone` event. Button labels animate by `text-shadow: 0 var(--text-offset) currentColor` — the duplicate slides up into place.
- Hero [verified]: eased mouse at lerp `0.025` (position) and `0.01` (pace); `movement.intensity 0.075`; camera offset `mouse * 0.02 * helmetRevealValue`, with the Y axis applied only above 768 px. Hover tweens run `0.5 s power4.inOut`. Scroll scrubs camera z `0 → -1` and a `uFilter` uniform `0 → 1` across one viewport height on `[data-gl-track="head"]`.
- Shaders and post [verified]: a fluid-cursor simulation rendering to its own target and fed in as `tCursorEffect`; a simplex-noise idle displacement and a background-noise target so nothing is ever static; a time-driven scanline shader over a wireframe helmet built by merging the GLB's child geometries; `UnrealBloomPass` behind an `EffectComposer`. Head-scene knobs, verbatim: `THICKNESS 0.000005`, `DISTORT_INTENSITY 0.5`, `NOISE_DETAIL 3`, `CURSOR_INTENSITY 0.15`, `CURSOR_SCALE 3`, `CURSOR_BOUNCE -0.75`, `REVEAL_SIZE 25`, `REVEAL_DURATION 1.1`. Custom fragment shaders end with `#include <colorspace_fragment>`.
- Time of day [verified]: the helmet variant is not fixed — `gl.time.getVariantAccordingToTime()` picks Lime, Dark or Grid from the visitor's clock, in both the hero and the heroflip scene.
- `helmet-scroll` [verified]: a paused GSAP timeline — `helmetGroup.rotation.y` `π/2.2 → 4π` over the whole scrub, with `helmet.rotation.x` swinging `π/12 → -π/10 → π/20` across the two halves on `power1.in` / `power1.out` — whose `progress()` is set from a scroll-written `PROGRESS` param each frame.
- Heroflip [verified]: a gold helmet on a MotionPathPlugin Bézier, gated by a `[data-heroflip="track"]` ScrollTrigger with `refreshPriority: -99`.
- 404 [verified]: a 20° camera on a helmet scaled ×6, auto-rotating, with an accumulating azimuthal-angle counter and a smoothstep vertical fade on the output plane. (Earlier "dual-quaternion" wording came from the rebuild; the live code uses an azimuth accumulator.)
- Sound [unknown — no audio API, `.mp3` or `.wav` reference in the fetched source].

## 5. Tech and pipeline
- Shell/engine split [verified]: Webflow owns markup, SEO, images and hosting; one self-hosted esbuild bundle owns every behaviour. Deployed independently — and visibly so (see §6).
- Assets [verified]: DRACO-compressed GLBs (`helmet-21.glb`, `disco-02.glb`, `tracks/tracks.glb`), RGBE HDRIs, WebP textures throughout, MSDF font atlases; `preconnect` + `dns-prefetch` to the Webflow CDN; a `preload` on the Mona Sans variable woff2; Vimeo loaded lazily by id (`data-stream-url="824804225"`).
- Texture tiering: **all texture paths in the live manifest are `/webp/`.** The only KTX2/Basis code in the bundle is GLTFLoader's inert `KHR_texture_basisu` extension. The "desktop WebP vs ≤991 px KTX2" tier recorded from the rebuild is **not present on the live site** [verified, engine-main.js].
- Debug budget [verified]: `?debug` also gates *loading* — two extra head-shadow textures and the dark HDRI are only fetched when the flag is set. Cheap and worth copying.
- Resize [verified]: crossing 992 px in either direction calls `window.location.reload()` after a 150 ms debounce. Scenes resize within a tier but not across one.
- Orientation [verified]: on a phone in landscape a `mob-landscape` Rive panel is shown via `screen.orientation` change events instead of a landscape layout.
- No-GL path [verified]: `isWebGL2Available()` is checked; on failure the engine is never constructed and `gl-fallback` is added to `<html>`.
- Performance [unknown]: the capture manifest's LCP figures are a headless SwiftShader cold-cache artefact and are not a claim about the site. Awwwards' jury scored WPO 7.60 [verified, entry]. `domNodes` 1957 desktop / 1889 mobile, 21 canvases, `cls: 0`, zero console errors and zero page errors across all three capture passes [verified, manifest.json]; the only failed requests were analytics beacons blocked in the headless context.

## 6. Weaknesses
- Reduced motion: **no `prefers-reduced-motion` anywhere** — zero matches in the served CSS and zero in the engine bundle [verified]. `desktop-rm-s00.png` and `desktop-rm-s50.png` are readable and nothing is stranded at opacity 0, but the noise field is in a different state between the two s00 frames, so the idle animation keeps running under the preference. It passes by luck, not by design. *We branch: a reduced tier that freezes the fluid sim and the idle noise to a still, keeps the persona fork and the scrubbed helmet reachable, and a static tier with no canvas at all.*
- Development scripts in production [verified, index.html lines 29–45]: the served document ships `<script defer src="http://localhost:6645/app.js">` plus four `dev-js/` paths, three of which now answer `403 Access denied - Invalid referrer` to a plain fetch. A hard-coded `localhost` script tag on a Site-of-the-Day homepage is a mixed-content request that fails for every visitor. *We ship a build step that strips dev origins and fails the release if a non-production host appears in the document.*
- Semantics [verified, index.html]: one `<main>`, twelve `<section>`, one `<h1>`, but **no `<nav>`, `<header>` or `<footer>` elements**; only `role="list"`/`"listitem"` appear in the markup. Awwwards docked exactly here — Semantics/SEO 7.40 and Markup/Meta-data 7.40, the two lowest developer marks after Accessibility [verified, entry]. *We use real landmarks; a `<div>` with a role is the fallback, not the plan.*
- Accessibility is real but partial [verified]: a global lime `:focus-visible` ring, a `[screen-reader]` utility, screen-reader clones for every split headline, `aria-label` / `-expanded` / `-controls` / `-pressed` set at runtime, `aria-current="page"`, alt text on all 133 images, and 17 `keydown` handlers. Awwwards still scored Accessibility 7.00, its lowest developer criterion [verified, entry]. The MSDF headlines inside GL have no DOM mirror [verified, scene text comes from JS string params].
- Breakpoint reload [verified]: a 992 px crossing reloads the page — an admission that six scenes are not resize-elastic, and a trap for zoom users. *We rebuild on `ResizeObserver` and never reload.*
- Mobile legibility [verified, mobile-s50.png]: at 390 px the lime "ON" scribble overlays and partly hides both the "TRACK" heading and the sentence under it. The phone layout is otherwise properly designed — stacked columns with explicit lime arrow buttons replacing the desktop hover, and a "TAP TO…" affordance pinned bottom-right on the hero [verified, mobile-s00.png].
- Dead code shipped [verified]: `scene.remove(Q.name)` passes a string where an `Object3D` is required, so that disposal branch silently does nothing; the nav-theme code prefers `window.ScrollTrigger || window.gsap?.ScrollTrigger`, neither of which the bundle ever assigns, so the rect-measuring fallback always runs; `--nav-height: calc(3.75rem (var(--gap) * 2))` is invalid CSS and resolves to nothing; one helper picks a scroller with `window.innerWidth <= 991 ? "body" : "body"`; `helmetMaterial.iridescence = 0` is set in every variant branch and has no effect unless the material is `MeshPhysicalMaterial` [inferred].
- Load gate [verified]: real asset readiness with no timeout, no skip and no repeat-visit shortcut.

## 7. Principles (3–6, generalisable)
1. Put the metaphor in the rendering pipeline. If the idea is "X seen as Y", make the shader do Y; do not caption it.
2. Tether the canvas to the layout, not to a second scroller: give every scene its own render target, then position its plane from DOM bounds plus the smooth-scroll offset. Text stays text, geometry stays canvas, and they never drift.
3. Derive the whole type scale from one formula anchored to the real comp width, and re-anchor it per breakpoint rather than rewriting the sizes.
4. Let one variable change with the world — time of day, season, a live result — so the page is not identical for two visitors. One switch is enough.
5. Use two animation runtimes for what each does best: authored 2D vector states in one, spatial and scroll-linked work in the other, and never force one to do the other's job.
6. Boring platform, uncompromised engine: a CMS owns content, one self-hosted bundle owns motion, and they deploy independently — which also means the release checklist has to cover both.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:**
  - The shell/engine split — CMS or static host for content and SEO, one ESM bundle for all motion, served from your own origin.
  - Render-to-texture per scene, composited onto DOM-positioned planes in a single shared scene, gated on a per-scene in-view ScrollTrigger so off-screen scenes cost nothing.
  - The asset-gated preloader — `Promise.all([vectorReady, glReady])` then reveal — plus the timeout and skip control the original lacks.
  - The persona-fork IA: split one subject into two opposed routes and make the homepage stage the choice.
  - Fluid type from one baseline: `clamp(min, 100vw, max) / design-width * design-unit`, re-declared per breakpoint.
  - MSDF text for headlines that live in 3D (atlas + JSON per face) — crisp at any scale — **with a DOM mirror**, which this site omits.
  - Interleaving a display serif and a heavy grotesque *inside* one headline, word by word.
  - A time-of-day (or otherwise live) variant switch on the hero's key material.
  - `?debug` gating asset *loading*, not just a GUI: the debug-only textures never reach a visitor.
  - Screen-reader clones and `aria: "auto"` on every split headline; a global `:focus-visible` ring in the accent colour.
  - Curved, notched and masked section edges instead of straight rules; SVG `mask-image` from the brand's own silhouette.
  - Rive artboards scrubbed by ScrollTrigger through a numeric state-machine input.
- **Don't take:**
  - The helmet, the visor/scanline hero, the wireframe-driver signature, the gold heroflip, the orbiting-helmet 404, the disco mode.
  - The on-track / off-track route names and the seven-route order.
  - The lime `#d2ff00` + near-black `#111112` pair as a package, the Mona Sans + Brier pairing, or 1728 as a number.
  - The 992 px reload, and the absent reduced-motion branch.
  - `<div role="list">` in place of real landmarks — it is exactly what the developer score punished.
  - Anything resembling a `localhost` script tag in a shipped document.
