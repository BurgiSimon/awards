# Corpus index — analysed award-winning sites, plus one clone-level reference

Use this table to pick **neighbours** for a new brief: the three cards closest by class, visitor mode, WebGL dosage and budget. Read those three cards in full, write down the principle each one gives you and the literal move you refuse to copy (the DIVERGENCE block of the direction contract). Everything here is a pattern citation, never an instruction to reproduce a site.

Confidence is the card's overall label. Since the 0.2 pass it means: **high** = the live site was
rendered and its source read, and its award entry was read from the entry page itself; **medium** =
search extraction of award pages and galleries; **low** = model recall only. Rows still marked
medium or low have not had their live pass yet — the repository's [verification log](https://github.com/burgisimon/awards/blob/main/docs/handoff/verification-log.md)
says which.

`floema-jewelry` is not an award reference and carries no verified score: it is the clone-level
material that was filed under `floema` until the 2026-09-18 pass showed that slug belongs to a
different company. It is cited as `[clone:floema-jewelry]`, never `[site:…]`, so each of its citations shows
its evidence level where it is read (`lint-refs.mjs` enforces the form), and it never fills a DIVERGENCE slot.

## Picking neighbours

1. **Match the class first** (portfolio, studio, B2B product, campaign, brand, docs, e-commerce). A B2B hardware brief has `seasats`, `usavionix`, `united-carriers` and `white-desert` as its natural set; a portfolio has `leo-parpeix`, `the-line`, `lama-lama`.
2. **Then the visitor mode.** Persuade sites carry conversion; experience sites carry a world; read sites must stay scannable under any effect (`shopify-editions-w26`, `animejs`).
3. **Then the WebGL dosage you can afford.** `the-line` took Site of the Day DOM-first; `igloo` took Site of the Day 100 % canvas (its entry shows no Site of the Year, whatever the card once claimed); `seasats` spends its WebGL on one globe and paints the rest into 2D canvases. Dosage is a budget decision, not a quality signal.
4. **Then the scroll model.** Native + smooth library is the default; virtual float only when the story must gate or hold (`why-zero`, `igloo`, `floema`); section switcher for short loops (`slosh-seltzer`).
5. Read the three cards' §7 (principles) and §8 (take / don't take); copy nothing from §1–§4 literally.

## Calibration rows

Every figure is read from a live Awwwards entry and recorded on its card; `node scripts/calibration.mjs --since 2021` recomputes this section from the cards, so refresh it each quarter and after every wave. Across the 31 entries awarded since 2021 [verified per card; counted by `scripts/calibration.mjs --since 2021`, 2026-10-22], Site of the Day overalls run **7.19 to 8.18**, median 7.62. Per axis: Design 7.13–8.12, Usability 7.00–7.90, Creativity 7.21–8.71, Content 7.16–8.18; the developer mean 6.83–8.03.

**Usability is the lowest of the four axes on 27 of the 31 entries.** The four exceptions miss by hundredths: `[site:white-desert]` (Creativity 7.21 under Usability 7.27), `[site:noth]`, `[site:aardvarkbookclub]` and `[site:bethebuzz]`. Creativity is the highest axis on concept-led winners (`[site:lando-norris]` 8.71, `[site:oryzo]` 8.35, `[site:slosh-seltzer]` 8.30, `[site:son-daven]` 8.15). Every entry publishes a developer score, so the technical bar is not optional. Of the six published developer criteria, **Accessibility is the one corpus sites score worst on**: the best Accessibility score on the 30 cards that publish it is 7.60, median 6.80, where the other five criteria reach 8.00 to 9.60. It is not out of reach: Cartier's Site of the Day of 20 Oct 2026, not a corpus card, scored 8.00 [verified, awwwards.com/sites/cartier-le-choeur-des-pierres read 2026-10-22]. The reduced-motion, keyboard and DOM-mirror paths are where new work can beat the reference set rather than copy it — see `references/jury/rubric.md` for the full derivation and for why this plugin's own jury scores about 0.7 below the award.
