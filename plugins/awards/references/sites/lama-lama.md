## 1. Concept and narrative
- **The one idea:** an agency that argues by tone of voice. The h1 states the thesis — "A creative digital agency that goes all in or not at all." [verified, index.html] — and every piece of chrome then tells a joke, so the visitor is being auditioned for a culture, not shown a process.
- **The signature interaction is a copy randomiser.** Each chapter carries a `data-labels` attribute with three to five alternative headings separated by `[/split/]`, and the sticky nav pill picks one per load: the hero offers "NICE ENTRANCE", "LET'S DO DAMAGE", "YOU MADE IT", "LOOKING SHARP TODAY"; the case chapter offers "OUR GREATEST HITS" or "CASES WITH TEETH"; the contact chapter ends on "BRING SNACKS" [verified, index.html; three capture runs show three different draws — desktop-s00, desktop-rm-s00, mobile-s25]. The site is therefore never twice the same read, at zero layout cost.
- **The hero media is randomised too.** Three capture runs produced three different full-bleed macro clips — an organic blue-and-gold structure, skin, an iris [verified, desktop-s00.png / mobile-s00.png / desktop-rm-s00.png]; the hero block holds several HLS playlists and the bundle picks with `Math.random()` [verified, index.html + app-BjlihaiS.js].
- **Beats:** (1) hero — macro footage under a three-line display headline, an intro paragraph right, a "THIS IS US" portrait card top-right; (2) cases — a run of named rows, each with tag chips and a horizontal image rail; (3) services; (4) clients — a logo marquee under `[ HAPPY CLIENTS ]`; (5) team and culture, photography-led; (6) contact — "BRIEF US SOMETHING." with a phone chip and two buttons [verified, desktop-s00/25/50/75/100.png].
- **Tone:** value-led copy in the body, deadpan comedy in the labels. The 2021 entry's positioning line about clients with a conscience belongs to the previous `.nl` site, not this one [verified, Awwwards entry read 2026-09-18].

## 2. Structure and components
- **Chrome that never leaves:** a centred, frosted, pill-shaped nav bar (logo mark · rotating chapter label · hamburger), and on desktop a right-edge stack of collapsible cards — "GET IN TOUCH" (with a team avatar), "THIS IS US", "PITCHDECK", "OUR AWWWARDS TALK" — each opening a thumbnail in place [verified, all desktop captures; `stacking_cards` chunk]. The mobile frames drop the stack entirely and keep only the pill [verified, mobile-s00/25/100.png].
- **The logo mark swaps per chapter** (`data-logo="logo"` on most, `data-logo="smile"` on services), and the services chapter shows a pixel-grid smiley in the pill [verified, index.html + desktop-s50.png].
- **Case index:** not poster blocks. Each project is a full-width row — title left in the grotesque, mono tag chips beneath, a `( + )` or `( VIEW + )` affordance, and a horizontal rail of stills and video that runs off the right edge [verified, desktop-s25.png, mobile-s25.png]. ## 3. Visual language
- **Grounds:** near-black `#1A1C1C` dominates; `#F9F4EB` and the four accents are per-section swaps through the semantic tokens [verified, main-FSCSogN-.css]. The captured home page runs dark end to end with the media supplying all the colour [verified, desktop-s00…s100.png].
- **Type:** the grotesque is set oversized, tight and all-caps for display, sentence case for body; the mono carries every functional string, always bracketed or chipped — `[ WE ARE LAMA LAMA ]`, `[ HAPPY CLIENTS ]`, `BRANDING`, `( VIEW + )` [verified, captures + index.html]. That mono/grotesque split, not a serif pairing, is the actual type contract.
- **Grid:** 12 columns desktop / 6 mobile, gutter 1.5rem / 0.625rem, margin 2.5rem / 1rem; content widths expressed as 8-of-12 and 6-of-12 column spans; spacing and section scales are `max(rem, vw)` pairs locked to a 1440 artboard, from 10/20px up to 160/320px [verified, main-FSCSogN-.css].
- **Materials:** heavy `backdrop-filter` blur (4 / 16 / 64px) on the pill and the card stack, and one `mix-blend-mode: exclusion` [verified, main-FSCSogN-.css]. Imagery is human and documentary — macro footage, portraits, on-set stills; no renders, no illustration [verified, captures].
- **Browser surfaces:** `theme-color` and `msapplication-TileColor` both `#000000`, a full favicon and webmanifest set, a custom scrollbar component [verified, index.html].

## 4. Motion and effects (with parameters)
- **Easing vocabulary is two curves.** Of 142 named GSAP tweens, `power4.out` appears 61 times and `expo.out` 58 — 84% of the total. The remainder is a long tail: `power4.inOut` 6, `power3.out` 4, `power4.in` 3, `none` 3, `power3.in` 2, `sine.out` 2, `power2.out` 2, `power1.out` 1 [verified, app-BjlihaiS.js].
- **Duration band is tight.** 0.65s (40 uses), 0.45s (20), 0.35s (17), 0.55s (16), 1.0s (8), 0.85s (7), 0.75s (7), 0.5s (6). The only long values belong to the intro: 2.75s, 3.25s, 3.75s, 4.25s [verified, same file].
- **Reveals are clip-path wipes, not splits.** `clipPath: inset(0% 0% 100% 0%)` → `inset(0% 0% 0% 0%)` for line containers, `inset(0% 100% 0% 0%)` for horizontal wipes, and `-10%` overshoot insets on scaled elements so nothing crops at the edge; there is no SplitText and no `stagger:` anywhere — sequencing is authored as explicit timeline positions plus `data-delay="0.1"` / `"0.2"` in the markup [verified, app-BjlihaiS.js + index.html].
- **Card show/hide:** `scale 1 → 0.96`, `yPercent 0 → -80`, `clipPath inset(110% -10% -10% -10%)`, `expo.out`, 0.65s out / 0.85s in [verified, app-BjlihaiS.js].
- **Intro:** `power4.in` 0.45s on the progress bar, `power3.in` 3.75s growing the logo pixel grid, `power3.in` 2.75s on logo progress, `power4.inOut` 4.25s on the colour crossfade [verified, same file].
- **The shader layer does the heavy work.** 39 uniforms across eight GLSL ES 3.00 programs: velocity and scroll (`u_velocity`, `u_delta`, `u_scroll`, `u_parallax_progress`), image planes tethered to DOM boxes (`u_content`, `u_content_dimensions`, `u_position`, `u_size`, `u_scale`, `u_mirror`, `u_previous`, `u_render_content`), hover distortion (`u_image_hover`, `u_distort_content`), a WebGL cursor (`u_cursor`, `u_cursor_theme`, `u_nocursor`), a pixel/dither grid (`u_pixel_size`, `u_nogrid`, `u_nogrid_progress`), theme crossfade (`u_theme`, `u_theme_second`, `u_color_progress`) and transition progress (`u_in_progress`, `u_out_progress`, `u_middle_progress`, `u_reveal_progress`) [verified, app-BjlihaiS.js].
- **Text flicker:** a character randomiser (`Math.random() * this.charLength`) rather than a GSAP ScrambleText plugin; the footer stat is caught mid-flicker in the first frame [verified, app-BjlihaiS.js + desktop-s00.png].
- **Page transitions:** Swup with `data-swup-morph` on six persistent elements, so the pill, the card stack and the footer survive a route change [verified, index.html].
- **Lenis configuration** (lerp, wheel multiplier, duration) is not recoverable from the minified call site [unknown].

- **Weights, as served:** the eager app chunk is 853 KB raw / **234 KB brotli over the wire**, the stylesheet 99.6 KB raw (≈15 KB gz), the server-rendered HTML 437 KB raw (≈34 KB gz) [verified, fetched files + `content-encoding: br`, `content-length: 234175`]. Assets carry `cache-control: max-age=31536000`.
- **Video pipeline:** 24 `<video>` elements, all `preload="none"`, `autoplay loop muted playsInline`, `opacity:0` with a fade-in flag, sources held in `data-src` and attached lazily; 29 `.m3u8` playlists on Bunny Stream, played through hls.js [verified, index.html].
- **Images:** 131 `<img>`, every one with a non-empty `alt`, and the WebGL planes wrap a real responsive `<img>` with a full `srcset` ladder from 80w up [verified, index.html]. The DOM is a genuine mirror of what the canvas draws, which is the right way round.

- Layout stability measured at CLS ≈ 0.0004 desktop, 0 mobile [verified, manifest.json — headless SwiftShader, so treat as directional].

## 6. Weaknesses
- **Accessibility is the jury's lowest developer criterion at 6.60** [verified, Awwwards entry read 2026-09-18], and the source says why: zero occurrences of `prefers-reduced-motion` in either the stylesheet or the bundles; zero `focus-visible` rules; zero `aria-expanded` and `aria-current`; no focus trap; no skip link; no `<nav>` and no `<ul>`/`<li>` — 55 links and 39 buttons sit in a 1,626-`<div>` tree; and the viewport meta ships `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, which disables pinch-zoom [verified, index.html + main-FSCSogN-.css + app-BjlihaiS.js].
- **No reduced-motion path exists.** The three `desktop-rm-*` frames are pixel-comparable to their normal counterparts, which the zero source hits confirm is by omission rather than by a deliberate "animation is the content" choice [verified, captures + source]. It lands soft rather than broken: the reveals are opacity and clip-path tweens that complete, so nothing is left invisible at rest.
- **The virtual scroll costs the browser's own affordances.** With `html,body` fixed there is no native scrollbar (a custom one is shipped), no find-in-page scroll, and no scroll restoration [verified, main-FSCSogN-.css + `scrollbar` chunk]. Keyboard survives only because three listeners handle the arrow keys and Enter — the whole 853 KB bundle contains two `keydown` handlers and one `keyup` [verified, app-BjlihaiS.js].
- **The entry payload is heavy** at 234 KB brotli before any lazy chunk, which is above the plugin's 200 KB gz entry-JS budget; WPO still scored 7.60, so the jury weighed caching and lazy media more than first-load bytes [verified, headers + entry].
- **Usability 7.36 is the lowest jury axis**, as on every scored site in this batch [verified, entry] — creativity is bought with an unconventional scroll and a chrome layer that hides content behind `( + )` toggles.
- **What the awards skills do differently:** `/awards:motion` writes the three reduced-motion tiers this site skips — hero video parked on a poster frame, clip-path wipes collapsed to opacity, the shader cursor and pixel grid disabled; `/awards:structure` keeps the case index a real `<ul>` of `<a>`s with `focus-visible`, gives the accordion cards `aria-expanded` and the menu a focus trap, and never ships `user-scalable=0`; `/awards:webgl` keeps the DOM mirror this site already gets right and adds the no-GL fallback; `/awards:stack` keeps a virtual scroll only with a keyboard and find-in-page contract written into AWARDS.md; `/awards:ship` holds the entry chunk to 200 KB gz.

## 7. Principles (3–6, generalisable)
1. **Randomise the copy, not the layout.** A small set of alternative labels per section, drawn at load, makes a static page feel authored for this visit — at no layout, no motion and no performance cost.
2. **Give the persistent chrome a job.** A pill that renames itself per chapter is wayfinding, brand voice and the section indicator in one element, which is why it can afford to be the only navigation on screen.
3. **Two curves and one duration band.** A whole site's motion can run on `expo.out` plus `power4.out` inside 0.35–0.85s; the vocabulary is what reads as considered, not the variety.
4. **Put the canvas under the DOM, not instead of it.** Render the images through the shader while the real `<img srcset alt>` stays in the markup: the effect is free of the accessibility and SEO bill it usually carries.
5. **Randomised media raises the ceiling on a single hero slot.** One layout plus a pool of clips means the second visit is a different site without a second design.
6. **Comedy is a differentiator a competitor cannot copy** — it is the one layer that reads as a specific team rather than a template.

## 8. Take / Don't take
- **Take:**
  - The per-section label pool with a random draw, wired to whatever chrome already persists; three to five variants per chapter is the observed density.
  - The two-curve, tight-band motion contract, and clip-path `inset()` wipes with `-10%` overshoot on anything that also scales.
  - The semantic colour layer (`--bgPrimary` / `--bgSecondary` / `--textPrimary` / `--signal`) re-pointed per block, so a section theme is one attribute rather than a component variant.
  - The grotesque-plus-mono contract, with the mono owning every functional string and always bracketed or chipped.
  - The video contract: `preload="none"` plus `data-src`, HLS through a streaming CDN, fade in from `opacity:0`, lazy per block.
  - The WebGL-over-real-`<img srcset alt>` pattern, and the lazy component graph mounted from `data-component` attributes.
  - A case index as rows with horizontal rails when the work is image-rich and the client wants breadth visible at a glance.
- **Don't take:**
  - `#1A1C1C` / `#F9F4EB` or Suisse BP Int'l as "the award look" — this batch already carries both, and [site:floema-jewelry] uses the same family.
  - The jokes themselves, the chapter label sets, or the pill-and-right-card-stack chrome as a shape.
  - The virtual scroll on a fixed `html,body` unless you are willing to rebuild the scrollbar, find-in-page, scroll restoration and the full keyboard path.
  - `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, the missing `prefers-reduced-motion` branch and the div-soup link tree — this is the 6.60 accessibility score, and it is the axis a new build should beat.
  - A 234 KB brotli entry chunk.
