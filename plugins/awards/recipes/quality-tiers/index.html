tier: …

/ probe once, then decide

Measure,
then spend.

The device is probed once (DPR, memory, cores, GPU string, WebGL2) and the first frames are timed. The result is a tier every effect reads: DPR cap, pixel budget, particle share, blur samples, post-processing on or off. Low tier gets a still, not a stutter.
