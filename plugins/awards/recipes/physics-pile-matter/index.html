physics-pile-matter

Tip the drawer out.

Fourteen pieces from a letterpress drawer fall into the box below once it scrolls into view, knock into each other, pile up and go still. Every piece is the list item itself: matter-js moves the bodies, the browser draws the words, so they stay text in reading order. The print shop and its drawer are a synthetic demo.

How the pile is built

  • matter-js steps on the shared ticker at a fixed 1/60 s, at most four steps a frame: no Runner, no second requestAnimationFrame.
  • Each list item is one body. Its position and angle are written to the item's transform, so the words stay selectable text in source order.
  • When every body sleeps the step leaves the ticker. A drag, a key, a tap or a change in the box's size wakes it, and a resize rebuilds the walls.
  • Under reduced motion no engine is created: the list wraps along the floor of the box, already at rest.
