/ persistent-mode-switch

Held by the visitor.

A mode the visitor chooses, not one the scroll imposes. The docked switch re-skins the page, the Grid pill lays the page's own twelve columns over it, and both remember the choice across reloads. Keys: L light, D dark, G grid. The copy and tiles are a synthetic demo.

Twelve tiles on twelve columns

01

Stored

The choice is written to localStorage and read back in the head, before first paint.

02

Shown

Each control prints its state in brackets, so the label is the readout.

03

Guarded

Single-key hotkeys ignore text fields and any Ctrl, Alt or Meta chord.

04

Constant

The chrome stays put while the ground and ink change beneath it.

05

Honest

Grid on shows the real columns these tiles sit in, not a decoration.

06

Calm

Under reduced motion the switch changes state with no tween at all.
