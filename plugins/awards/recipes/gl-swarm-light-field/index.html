gl-swarm-light-field · dom

The close
is a lit room.

Scroll to the footer. A swarm of instanced seeds drifts along a spiral path; a point light rides the pointer, and every seed shades its neighbours through a coarse voxel grid of where the swarm is. Nothing here is a model file.

Rules

  • The simulation runs on the CPU and writes positions and rotations into two float textures; one instanced draw reads them by instance index.
  • The swarm splats itself as one-pixel points into a 32 × 16 × 16 voxel grid stored as a sliced 2D atlas, with MAX blending; one pass blurs across slices and eases toward the new occupancy.
  • Each seed reads ambient occlusion one voxel along its normal and shadow one and two voxels toward the light.
  • The loop runs only while the footer is on screen. Reduced motion draws one settled frame with the light parked; the static tier draws nothing and the footer is plain DOM.

Parameters

  • 1,024 seeds on a desktop (32²), 576 on a phone or a low tier (24²); seed half-length 0.022 units.
  • Path: a diagonal across a landscape footer, a steep fall on a portrait one; curve amplitude 0.1 at frequency 8, spiral radius up to 0.2, drift 0.2 units a second, pull-back rate 0.5, damping 1.
  • Pointer: a sphere of radius 0.2 pushes at 0.25 and hands the seeds its own motion; the light floats 0.3 in front of the swarm and eases to the pointer at 12 per second.
  • Field: rise 0.15 and fall 0.05 a frame, so a seed that leaves a voxel keeps a short shadow behind it.
  • These are this recipe's own values. The corpus card names where the architecture came from; its numbers are not targets.

Accessibility and cost

  • The canvas is aria-hidden; every word and control in the footer is DOM above it, with 44 px targets.
  • Three.js is imported only when the footer is within a screen of the viewport, and the loop stops whenever the footer leaves it.
  • WebGL 2 is required (float data textures and MAX blending); without it the footer is plain DOM on its ground colour.
