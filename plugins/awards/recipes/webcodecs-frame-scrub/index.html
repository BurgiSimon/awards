One clip of a hundred and twenty frames, dawn to dusk. The scroll asks for a frame and WebCodecs decodes that frame and no other: from the nearest keyframe on a jump, one decode for the next frame, nothing at all for a frame it decoded a moment ago.

Rules

  • One file: the encoded chunks, a JSON manifest of every frame's offset, length, time and type, and a four-byte pointer to the manifest. One fetch, no demuxer.
  • A keyframe every ten frames, so a jump decodes ten frames at most; a step forward decodes one.
  • Seeks never reset the decoder: a request made while one is decoding waits, and only the newest runs.
  • Frames decoded on the way to a target are kept as bitmaps, so scrubbing back through them is free.
  • Every decoded frame is closed once it is drawn or kept.
  • Without WebCodecs the frames are drawn directly; with reduced motion the film is one still at midday.
