gl-grade-pass-scene-cuts · dom

Scene one · dusk

One canvas,
three worlds.

Every scene renders into the same target. The cuts between them are not a second scene composited on top: they are three lines in the last pass, where the grade already is.

Scene two · tide

The first cut is a blur that runs out of image.

Full-resolution blur first, then half, then quarter, then a flat colour. The scene swaps under the flat colour, where nothing can show the seam.

Scene three · moss

The second cut freezes the last frame.

On its first frame the cut copies the outgoing image. The new scene opens inside a growing diamond while the still stays outside, and a 2D canvas draws the diamond's edge in sync.

The close

The world closes to a window,
then to the ground colour.
