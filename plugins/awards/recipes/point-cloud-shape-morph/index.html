Rules

  • No WebGL: thousands of points are projected in JavaScript and drawn as small squares on a 2D canvas, far to near, in sixteen depth buckets.
  • Each shape is an SVG rasterised once; points are sampled from its opaque pixels. The scroll position picks the pair of shapes and the blend between them.
  • Each shape holds for the first and last 18 % of its segment, so a reader can see it; the swirl is weighted by sin²(πe) and is zero at rest.
  • Reduced motion: no travel. The two shapes cross-fade by opacity and the pointer does nothing.
