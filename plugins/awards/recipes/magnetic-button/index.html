Magnetic,
within reason.

The button leans toward a fine pointer inside a radius, its label leaning a little less than its ring so the two read as one object with depth. Outside the radius, and on every coarse pointer, it is an ordinary button.

Book a call

Rules

  • Pull only inside 1.6 × the button's diagonal; ease back with expo-out on leave.
  • Label moves at 0.45 of the ring's displacement.
  • gsap.quickTo on x/y: one tween per axis, retargeted every move, never a new tween per event.
  • Nothing pulls under (pointer: coarse) or prefers-reduced-motion; focus shows the ring at full strength.
