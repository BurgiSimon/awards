Leave a trail of pictures.

Move a mouse across this hero. A small picture lands every 80 pixels of travel, grows in, then fades in the order it came. Faster hands leave larger, tilted pictures. None of them ever covers this text.

Read the five rules

Five rules for a trail that stays polite

  1. Spacing is distance, not time: a picture every 80 px along the pointer's path, so a slow hand and a fast one leave the same density.
  2. Twelve pictures exist, ever. The thirteenth reuses the oldest; nothing is created while the pointer moves.
  3. Speed sets size (0.8× to 1.25×) and tilt (up to 10°), inside a clamp, so a flick reads as a flick without growing past the layout.
  4. The heading and the copy beside it are protected areas: a picture that would touch them is skipped.
  5. Touch screens and reduced motion get three still pictures placed by the design. The trail stops when the hero leaves the screen.
