How it moves

A cut, not a slide.

The marker is one element under the rows' text and over their plates, blended with multiply, so a plate under it takes the marker's colour while the paper around it does too. It moves by setting its offset to the row's own top, with no transition, which is why it reads as a gel laid down rather than a highlight chasing the pointer.

Keyboard focus moves it the same way, and on a touch screen it marks the row crossing a reading line two fifths of the way down the screen as you scroll.
