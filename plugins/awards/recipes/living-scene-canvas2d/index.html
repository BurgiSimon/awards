The close · synthetic demo

Scroll to the end. Something lives there.

The last strip of this page is a small scene drawn on one canvas. It moves a little on its own, it answers the pointer, and a click leaves something behind. Three scenes share one harness: a star chart, a lawn and a koi pond.

One harness, three scenes.

The harness owns the canvas, the clock and the input. A scene is one small module that seeds itself, draws a frame, and says what a pointer and a click mean to it.

The same picture on every load.

Stars, blades and fish are placed by a hash of their index, never by chance, so the scene is the same after a reload and after a resize. Only what the visitor does changes it.
