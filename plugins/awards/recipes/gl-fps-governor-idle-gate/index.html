Spend frames
on change.

The field behind this text is a synthetic demo. A governor samples the frame rate every half second and steps the pixel ratio down or up by a quarter, inside the range the device probe allowed. An idle gate stops drawing a moment and a half after your last scroll, pointer or key, unless a transition is still running.

What the loop does

Every frame adds its time to a half-second window. When the window closes, the average frame rate decides: under 45 the pixel ratio drops a step, over 57 it climbs one, and in between it holds. The steps never leave the range the one-time probe set.

Scroll, pointer, wheel, touch and key input wake the loop. When a moment and a half passes with no input, no running transition and no easing left to finish, the loop leaves the ticker and the canvas keeps its last frame. A hidden tab stops the ticker.
