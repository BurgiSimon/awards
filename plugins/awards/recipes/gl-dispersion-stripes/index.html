/ gl-dispersion-stripes · dom

/ synthetic demo

One stripe,
three phases.

A single procedural stripe is computed three times, once per colour channel, each with its phase nudged a little. Where the channels agree the metal reads grey; at every edge they part, and the fringe is the dispersion.

Rules

  • The stripe phase is a rotated ramp, bent by a radial bump and 2D simplex noise, and drifted by time.
  • Red and blue read the same stripe at a shifted phase; green reads it unshifted.
  • Each edge is anti-aliased from the phase, not the colour: coverage is re-thresholded by fwidth, guarded against zero.
  • Tone mapping, gamma and an interleaved-gradient-noise dither run inline: no render target.
  • Reduced motion and static: one still frame, no drift.
