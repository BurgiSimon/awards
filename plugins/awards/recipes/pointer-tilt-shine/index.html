Cards that lean into the pointer.

Rules

  • The face turns toward the pointer: the corner under it sinks, at most 7° on each axis, through gsap.quickTo.
  • A shine follows the pointer across the face and a shade gathers on the far side, both in step with the tilt.
  • Scrolling locks every card where it is; the next pointer move after a 140 ms quiet period unlocks them.
  • Mouse pointers only. Touch, pen, keyboard and reduced motion get a flat card with no layers.
