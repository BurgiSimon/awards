01 / Poster

A single thought, held at scale.

Made to stay in use.

Typography, paying attention

A bench earns its place through ordinary use. The seat softens at its edges; the grain grows familiar. We leave the joints accessible so a worn part can be replaced, and the useful whole can remain.

  • Solid white oak, with visible grain.
  • Wooden pins at each leg joint.
  • A matte surface, renewed with oil.
