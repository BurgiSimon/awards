gl-pointer-wake-reveal · dom

Under the paper

Move slowly.
Something is awake.

The page is paper. Where the pointer passes, a wake opens it onto a night scene for three quarters of a second, then the paper closes again. The scene renders at a reduced scale, every second frame, and not at all while nothing is open.

Rules

  • The wake is up to 140 points, each living 750 ms, painted as radial gradients into a 320 × 200 canvas that the shader reads as a mask.
  • Faster movement opens a wider wake: radius 9.5 % of the short side × (0.6 + 0.6 × speed / 1,600 px/s).
  • Fine pointers only. On touch, under reduced motion or without WebGL there is only paper, which is the complete page.
