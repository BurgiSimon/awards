Harbour Lane Studio

About

Harbour Lane is a six-person design and engineering studio working from a converted net loft. We make identities, websites and printed matter for organisations that would rather be understood than admired.

Most projects start with a week of reading: annual reports, complaint letters, old brochures, the way reception answers the phone. The visual work follows from what that reading turns up, which is why no two of our sites share a template.

We build what we design. The same people who draw the type scale write the scroll code, so a transition is never a line in a hand-off document that nobody owns.

Selected clients

A regional ferry operator, two independent publishers, a seed library, a lighthouse trust, a cooperative of glassblowers, a night-bus campaign and a museum of weather instruments.

How we work

Fixed fees, agreed in writing before any drawing starts. One weekly call, one shared document of decisions, and a working prototype by the third week so opinions are formed on the real thing.

We keep projects small enough that the founders stay on them from first meeting to launch, and we say no to work we cannot staff that way.

Every site we ship passes a keyboard walk, a reduced-motion walk and a phone walk before the client sees it, and again the day it goes live.

Recognition

Our work has been shortlisted by a handful of design annuals. We mention it here because clients ask, not because it changes the fee.
