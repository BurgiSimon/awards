Rules

  • Neighbours dim to .2 and blur 6px; the centred card is sharp. Below 920px there is no blur and neighbours sit at .1.
  • The gap is computed so a fixed slice of each neighbour shows: 10 % of the width on desktop, 14 % on a phone.
  • The release speed picks the snap: a fast flick lands in 320 ms on a crisp curve, a slow drag in 450–600 ms on a softer one.
  • Past either end the drag resists, and a critically damped spring on the shared ticker brings it back.
  • Arrow keys, Home and End step the carousel; reduced motion keeps the blur but snaps instantly.
