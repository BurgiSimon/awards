/ one renderer, six canvases

One context.
Six small canvases.

Each specimen below is its own button with its own 2D canvas, laid out by the grid. A single offscreen WebGL renderer draws them one at a time and copies each frame into its canvas, only when that specimen changed and sits near the viewport. Hover or press one to turn it.

Rules

  • One WebGLRenderer on a detached canvas. Each item gets a viewport-sized slice of its drawing buffer, a render, and a drawImage into the item's own 2D canvas in the same task.
  • An item re-renders only when it is dirty (its turn moved, a resize, a theme change) and an IntersectionObserver says it is within half a viewport of the screen.
  • One pixel budget for the set: pixel ratio = min(tier DPR, √(budget / (item area × count))).
  • Teardown disposes every geometry and material, then renderer.dispose() and forceContextLoss(). The 2D canvases keep their last frame.
