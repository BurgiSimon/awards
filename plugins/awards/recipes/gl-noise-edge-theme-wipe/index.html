A new ground, poured from where you pressed.

Press Dark ground. A full-screen quad grows the next ground colour from the press point with a ragged, noisy edge. When it covers every corner, the page's tokens flip underneath it and the cover fades away. The copy on this page is a synthetic demo.

Four rules for a covered swap

Under full cover

The tokens change only once the edge has passed the farthest corner, so no frame ever shows half of one theme and half of the other.

From the press point

The circle starts at the pointer. A key press starts it at the button's centre instead. Two octaves of hash noise roughen the rim.

Kept for the visit

The choice goes to sessionStorage and a head script restores it before first paint. A new tab starts from the default ground.

Calm when asked

Under reduced motion the swap is instant and no canvas frame is drawn. Between swaps the canvas is hidden and draws nothing.

A reading ground is a choice, not a mood.

Some visitors read long pages at night, on a bright screen, in a dark room. A second ground is a courtesy to them; a wipe that takes a second is a courtesy to everyone else, because it makes the change legible instead of abrupt. The cover hides the moment the tokens swap, which is the one moment that would otherwise flicker.

Press the button again during the cover and the same edge turns around and shrinks back to where it started. Nothing was swapped yet, so nothing needs undoing.
