/ mass

A dot and a ring,
two speeds.

The dot follows the pointer almost instantly (lerp .75), the ring lags (lerp .22). The gap between them is what a visitor reads as mass. Over interactive elements the ring grows and the dot shrinks; over tagged elements a badge names the affordance. On coarse pointers none of this exists.
