/ a small-press bindery

We setproofbindshipset books for small presses.

Driver

Reduced motion: the first word holds.

One word in a fixed headline turns through a list. Timed rolls it on a clock with a small overshoot; typed erases and retypes it per character; wheel-stepped spends the first wheel inputs on the word before the page moves. The slot is as wide as its widest word, so the line never reflows. The bindery and its copy are a synthetic demo.

After the hero.

Once the last word has landed, the next wheel input scrolls the page as usual. Keyboard and touch scrolling never wait on the word.
