/ virtual float · 000

Scroll is
a camera.

The document does not scroll. Wheel and touch move a float with friction; the float sets the camera's position on a spline; labels are anchored to points in the scene. Idle for a moment and the float snaps to the nearest chapter. Keys move by chapter; reduced motion steps without easing.
