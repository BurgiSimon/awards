/ 01 · Tidewater survey

Four stations, one shoreline, read at low water.

The Tidewater survey logs the same four stations every spring tide. Each visit records the waterline, the shell count in a one-metre square and the colour of the sand at a hand's depth.

Rules

  • Only a wheel arms the settle. A resting position is judged after 160 ms without movement, counted on the shared ticker.
  • Within 6 % of either end of the zone nothing happens; otherwise the page eases to the nearer end over .7 s, ease-out cubic.
  • A 900 ms guard, longer than the tween, stops the settle from re-arming on its own motion.
  • A pressed pointer holds everything and stops a settle in place. Keys, find-in-page, focus scrolls and touch never arm it.
  • Off on coarse pointers and under reduced motion, where the wheel is also instant.
