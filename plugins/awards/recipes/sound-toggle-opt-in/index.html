/ 01 · consent

Sound waits
to be asked.

Nothing here creates an AudioContext until a visitor presses the switch. The bed and the click are synthesised — oscillators, filtered noise, one analyser — so the page ships no audio file and the first gesture is the first sound.

Ambient bed
two detuned sines under filtered noise, gain .375
Click
one-shot blip, gain .35
Stored
awards:sound in localStorage, read in a try/catch

/ 02 · the three states

Off, armed, on.

Off is the default and the honest one: no context, no nodes, nothing suspended in the background. Armed is what a returning visitor gets — the stored yes is remembered, the audio still waits for a gesture, because the browser and the visitor both insist on one. On is the only state that makes a sound, and it is the only state that has ever touched the audio hardware.

The switch is a real <button>: its name carries the state, aria-pressed carries it again for assistive tech, and Enter or Space works because nothing intercepts the keyboard. The 24 × 24 meter beside the label is decoration over that button, driven by an AnalyserNode; under reduced motion it freezes in the on pose and never subscribes to a clock.

/ 03 · leaving the tab

Hidden is silent.

A bed that keeps playing in a background tab is the fastest way to be closed. visibilitychange suspends the context and the meter stops; coming back resumes both, and only if the switch was on when you left.
