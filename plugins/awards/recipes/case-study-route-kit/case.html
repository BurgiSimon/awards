The brief

The market reopened after two years under scaffolding with the same sixty-four stalls and none of the old signs. Traders were asked the same three questions forty times a morning: where the cheese is, where the fish is, and where the way out is.

We named the six aisles after what was sold in them when the hall opened, painted stall numbers at eye height, and hung one marker per aisle where it can be read from the gate, thirty metres away.
