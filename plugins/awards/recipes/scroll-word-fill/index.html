/ word fill

Read it as you scroll it.

Below, a statement holds still on a sticky stage while the scroll brings each word from half ink to full, one after another, so the sentence reads at the speed of the reader's hand. This page and its type foundry are a synthetic demo.

/ the foundry

We cut letters the slow way: drawn at the size they will be read, proofed on paper, spaced by eye, and only then set loose on a screen where every word has to earn the next one.

After the fill.

The stage releases once the last word lands, with a short hold at full ink so the whole sentence can be read at rest.
