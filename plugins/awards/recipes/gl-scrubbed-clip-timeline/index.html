Three forms, grown by the scroll.

Each form carries an animation clip built in code from keyframe tracks: petals that open, discs that stack, beads that coil. Nothing plays on a clock. One scrubbed timeline sets every clip's time from the scroll position, so scrolling back ungrows them. The forms and the copy are a synthetic demo.

How the clock is wired

  • Each form is a group of generated meshes with one AnimationClip made of position, rotation and scale tracks. No model or texture file is loaded.
  • One timeline of nine units gives each form a slot of three: a hand-off that slides the rail, a growth that drives mixer.setTime, and a tone that warms the material.
  • ScrollTrigger scrubs that timeline from the top of the track to its bottom; Lenis runs on the GSAP ticker.
  • A frame is drawn only when a scroll update or a resize has changed something. At rest the canvas draws nothing.
  • Under reduced motion each form appears settled, cut at the slot boundary. Without WebGL2 a CSS still stands in.
