A valley,
slowly.

Every page change here goes through one curtain: a grid of cells that grow from hairlines into solid bars, swept from the far corner, so the page weaves shut, changes, and weaves open again.

Rules

  • The grid is rebuilt to the viewport: cells about 72 × 75 CSS px, so a phone gets fewer, taller columns.
  • Each cell scales on X from a hairline to full width in 0.4 s; the sweep starts at the bottom-right cell, 0.02 s apart by grid distance.
  • Arrival: fonts and image decodes, at least 0.9 s on a first visit and 0.25 s on a repeat, never more than 4 s.
  • Routes: the scroll is saved on leaving and restored only on back and forward; a new visit starts at the top.
  • On arrival the new page's heading takes focus, the title names it and a polite live region announces it.
  • Reduced motion: the curtain fades in 0.2 s instead of weaving.
