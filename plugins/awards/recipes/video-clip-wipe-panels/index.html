/ video-clip-wipe-panels

00 / Intro

Two layers, one edge.

Each panel owns a full-bleed clip. Changing panels wipes the next clip in over the current one behind a thin light seam. The clips and copy are a synthetic demo.

01 / Buffer

Load behind.

Only two media layers exist. The hidden one already holds the next panel's clip, so the wipe never waits on the network.

02 / Wipe

Open the gate.

The incoming layer opens from an inset clip in one second, and the seam rides its edge on the same tween. Going back, the wipe runs the other way.

03 / Calm

Or just fade.

Under reduced motion the clips rest on their first frame and panels cross-fade in a fifth of a second. Keys, buttons, wheel and swipe all still step.
