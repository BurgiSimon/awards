/ scrollbar-thumb-drag

/ thumb

A thumb you
can grab.

The drawn thumb on the right mirrors how far down the page you are and can be dragged. The browser's own scrollbar stays, so keys, find-in-page and assistive tech scroll exactly as before. This page and its copy are a synthetic demo.

/ 01

Mirror, don't replace.

The document scrolls natively. The thumb reads scrollY / (scrollHeight − innerHeight) once per frame and moves by transform; it never owns the scroll position.

/ 02

Drag writes the scroll.

Pointer down on the thumb captures the pointer; every move maps the thumb's offset over its free travel to a scroll position. Press on the track and the thumb centres under the pointer, then drags from there.

/ 03

Keys still work.

Space, Page Down, Home, End and the arrows move the page because nothing intercepts them. The thumb follows whatever moved the page, eased in the full tier.

/ 04

Reduced motion snaps.

Under prefers-reduced-motion the page scrolls natively without Lenis and the thumb sits exactly on the scroll position every frame, with no easing.

/ 05

Touch gets the native bar.

On coarse pointers the drawn track is hidden: a thumb too thin to hit is worse than the platform's own overlay scrollbar.
