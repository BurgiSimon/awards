Load, then arrive.

One move from loader to hero.

The curtain waits on real signals (the fonts and the hero's first frame), never on a timer, with a four-second ceiling. As it lifts, the hero opens from a rounded inset to full bleed while its media scales in, so loading and arriving read as one gesture. Scroll stays locked until the frame is open. A repeat visit in the same session skips straight to the open hero; under reduced motion the curtain only fades.
