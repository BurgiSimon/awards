Every fan opens from its centre.

Scroll, and the line opens like a fan: the middle letters barely move, the outer ones swing out and towards you. Scroll back, and every letter lands where it was set.Here the line stays as it was set: every letter keeps its place, and the page simply scrolls on to the bench below.

Three rules from the bench

  1. The pivot stays put. Every offset is a letter's distance from the headline's centre, raised to the power 1.6, so the letters at the pivot hardly travel and the ends take almost all of it.
  2. The ends travel furthest, and towards you. Each letter moves out along the line, comes forward in depth, turns on its upright and tips with its row, all from that one distance.
  3. Shut means shut. The scroll is the only clock. At the top of the page every letter's transform is removed, so the heading is plain text in its own layout again, and a narrower window splits it afresh.
