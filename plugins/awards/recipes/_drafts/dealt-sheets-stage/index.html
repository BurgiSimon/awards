Kessel Riso

Print studio · synthetic demo

Five runs off the drum, dealt one at a time.

Every print run leaves the studio as a pair of sheets: its job ticket and the print itself. Scroll, and each pair is dealt onto the mat, then swept off as the next one lands. The last pair stays where it falls.

The run sheets

Each ticket links to its run sheet: the paper, the inks, the order the drums went round.

  • Tidal Assembly. Pink first, so the sun sits under the waves where they cross.
  • Lantern Market. The blue master is cut with holes; the yellow drum fills them a hair off.
  • Field Recording Society. Rings at 30 % screen, type at full ink.
  • The Long Walk. Yellow path, blue arch, pink sun, each its own pass.
  • Small Press Fair. The second pass shifted by two millimetres and left that way.
