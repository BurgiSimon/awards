Wyke Hollow Pottery

Synthetic demo

Moon jar, an edition of twelve.

Porcelain, 41 cm tall, thrown as two bowls and joined at the waist. All twelve came out of one firing, and each carries its own warm cheek of ash.

Thrown as two bowls.

A jar this wide will not stand on the wheel in one piece, so each is thrown as two deep bowls of 6 kg each. They are joined rim to rim when leather-hard, and the join is turned smooth but never hidden: hold the jar to the light and the line runs round its waist.

Thirty-one hours in the kiln.

Wood-fired to 1,300 °C over a day and a night, with the firebox fed every eight minutes. The ash settles on the shoulder of whichever jar faces the flame, so no two in the edition carry the same warm cheek.

Jar seven is still unspoken for.

Eleven of the twelve have gone to the people who asked first. The last one ships in a crate of oat straw, with a card that names the firing and the shelf it stood on.

Write to the studio about jar seven

Wyke Hollow Pottery is a fictional studio. Its jar, its kiln and its numbers are synthetic demo content.

On a wide screen one drawing of the jar is carried from box to box as you scroll. With reduced motion, or on a narrow screen, each box shows its own.
