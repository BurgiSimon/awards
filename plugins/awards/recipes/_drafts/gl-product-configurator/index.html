How it is built

  • The lamp is a turned foot, a cylinder and a shade, all made in code. The three shades are one geometry with two morph targets, so changing the shade blends one shape into the next.
  • Each choice tweens the lacquer from where it is now, in four tenths of a second, so a second choice mid-way turns the first one around without a jump. The price and the line under it change at once.
  • The address bar holds the configuration, and the link under the options copies it: the same lamp comes back.
  • Frames are drawn only while the view turns or a material moves.
