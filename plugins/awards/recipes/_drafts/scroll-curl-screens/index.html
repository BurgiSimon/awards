Case notes

Tern
Built around one chart per harbour, cached for 40 days, so the tide is there with no signal.
Pilcrow
Columns are set by the editor, not the template; the paper's own rules for widows ship as checks.
Bramble
Every packet carries its grower's name back to the desk when it is returned in autumn.
Quay
Tickets are signed on the phone and checked on the gangway by a reader with no network.
Larch
Plots are drawn on the tablet in the wood and merged in the office in the evening.
