Case notes

Tern
Built around one chart per harbour, cached for 40 days, so the tide is there with no signal. Back to Tern
Pilcrow
Columns are set by the editor, not the template; the paper's own rules for widows ship as checks. Back to Pilcrow
Bramble
Every packet carries its grower's name back to the desk when it is returned in autumn. Back to Bramble
Quay
Tickets are signed on the phone and checked on the gangway by a reader with no network. Back to Quay
Larch
Plots are drawn on the tablet in the wood and merged in the office in the evening. Back to Larch
