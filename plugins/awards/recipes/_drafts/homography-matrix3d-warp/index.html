Loomtone is invented. The screen is live HTML, set into the drawing by one matrix.

Four corners in, one matrix out

A flat rectangle seen at an angle is a projective transform of itself, and a projective transform of the plane is pinned down by where four points land. The page reads the four corners of the drawn screen through the drawing's own scaling and crop, lerps each corner from the flat rectangle toward its target as you scroll, and solves the eight unknowns that send the rectangle to those four points.

The eight numbers go into the corners of a matrix3d with the origin at the element's top left. Nothing is rasterised and nothing is copied: the text stays text, the button stays a button, and the browser maps every click and every selection back through the same matrix.
