A pinned chapter, scrubbed.

One chapter pins for three viewport heights while a timeline scrubs through three beats: the object turns, the caption swaps, the readout counts. The scrub ease is none; the smoothing lives in the scroll, never in the tween.

Approach.Turn.Land.

Three beats; each holds for a third of the pin. Keyboard users scroll through with Space or PageDown; nothing is hijacked.

After the pin, the page resumes.

The chapter's own height is what the scroll consumes, so the browser's scrollbar stays truthful and the address of every beat is a real scroll position.
