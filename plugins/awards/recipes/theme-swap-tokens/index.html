/ theme-swap-tokens · default

/ 01 · default

Colour is
page state.

Each chapter names a theme. Crossing its midpoint tweens the tokens on <html> in one beat, and the canvas clear colour rides the same tween, so DOM and WebGL never disagree for a frame.

/ 02 · dark

Warm near-black,
bone ink.

The dark theme is not an inversion: it has its own accent and its own near-black, and the tween crosses through mixed values rather than snapping.

/ 03 · accent

Drenched
in one hue.

A full-bleed accent chapter is the loudest move available; it works because the page spends most of its length on the quiet grounds.

/ 04 · default

And back.

Scrolling up replays the swaps in reverse; nothing is left half-tweened because the tween is retargeted, not stacked.
