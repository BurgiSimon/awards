/ marquee-raf-mask

/ ticker

A marquee that
can stop.

A rAF translate with wraparound, not a CSS keyframe loop: it can pause on hover and focus, take its speed from scroll velocity, resume without a jump and stand still under reduced motion. The edges fade through a mask; the links inside stay real.

  • Seasats
  • White Desert
  • Montfort
  • The Line
  • Oryzo
  • Floema

Rules

  • The track is duplicated until it covers twice the viewport; the transform wraps at one copy's width.
  • Speed is 80 px/s plus |scroll velocity| × 0.6, damped, so fast scrolling flicks the band.
  • Pause on pointerenter and focusin; resume without a jump because the position is a float, not a keyframe time.
  • Under reduced motion the band is a static row that can be scrolled by hand.
