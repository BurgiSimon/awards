/ gl-video-dither-layers · dom

/ synthetic demo

Footage, woven
into the page.

Three short clips, each graded on black, are laid over one another on a single canvas. Black is the key, so every clip lands as a cut-out; everything else is read cell by cell and redrawn in the close's own ink, as a bar as wide as the cell is dark, or as a glyph from a ramp.

Rules

  • One canvas, one program, one quad per layer sized to the layer, so fill cost follows the footage, not the canvas.
  • Each layer carries its own cell grid, black and white points, bar range and ground and ink opacity; a black point above the white point inverts the tone.
  • The key is read at the cell centre and four points inside it, so silhouettes end on whole cells.
  • The layer settings are the tween targets: the scroll develops the levels from blank, a loop moves the flock across.
  • A clip uploads only when it has a new frame; the canvas renders only when something changed, at most 30 times a second, and never off screen.
  • Reduced motion and static: one developed still, the flock parked, no clock.
