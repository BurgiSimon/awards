/ one progress line · synthetic demo

Five scenes,
one line.

Scroll drives one value. Two bands cost more scroll, so their beats get time; each scene is switched on half-way through the one before and switched off behind; the clock that follows the scroll has a speed limit, so a flick cannot skip a beat.
