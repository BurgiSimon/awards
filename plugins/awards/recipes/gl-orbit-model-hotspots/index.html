Walk the site
before it exists.

A site model you can turn, inside a dialog. The orbit is clamped to a narrow band of composed views, the camera eases with framerate-independent damping, and every named zone is a real button that flies the camera to it.

Four zones, 3.2 ha.

Offices, a tower, a courtyard and parking. The same list lives in the dialog as the hotspot labels, so a keyboard or screen-reader visitor reaches every zone without touching the canvas.
