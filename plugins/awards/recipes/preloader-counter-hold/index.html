/ load

Count whatis loading.

The counter is tied to real signals (fonts, image decodes, a scene promise), advances in uneven jumps but never ahead of the truth, holds at 100 for a beat, and exits by lines. Repeat visits skip it through sessionStorage; a Skip button and an aria-live status keep it honest.
