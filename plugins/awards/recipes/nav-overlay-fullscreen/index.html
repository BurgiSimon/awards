A menu that
takes the screen.

Numbered links stagger in on their own theme; the page behind becomes inert and stops scrolling; focus is trapped, Escape closes, and focus returns to the button. Under reduced motion it simply appears.

Below the fold

Open the menu, press Tab a few times: focus never leaves the overlay. Press Escape: the page comes back exactly where it was.
