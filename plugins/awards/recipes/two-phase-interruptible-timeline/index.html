Close it
halfway open.

Open and close live in one paused timeline split by a pause at the hinge. Press the menu button twice quickly: the close rewinds what has already played instead of jumping to a separate exit, so the panel never lands half-built.

How to try it

Click the menu button, then click it again before the links settle. Once the links have settled, a close plays the second half forward. Escape closes and returns focus to the button.
