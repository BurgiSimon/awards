/ synthetic demo · 1 of 5 scenes

One weave,
every picture.

Five pictures down this page are woven by the same compositor on one WebGL context. Four clips are pooled between them: a clip three scenes show is one texture, uploaded once per frame.

/ 2 of 5

Weather

Each scene is an ordinary canvas in the flow of its section, so it scrolls with the page and sits under the section's text. The GL context draws it into a slice of a detached buffer and copies the slice across.

/ 3 of 5

Seasons

Both seasons are layers of the same scene. Switching does not cross-fade two pictures: the outgoing layers lose their ink and the incoming ones develop, in the section's own ink.
