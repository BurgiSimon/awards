Five panels, one gesture.

The page never scrolls. Wheel input fills the ring at the bottom; at full it commits one step and the next panel slides in. Stop short and the ring drains back after a second. The deck and its copy are a synthetic demo.

Accumulate.

Every wheel delta adds its magnitude to a running sum toward 500. The ring shows the sum, so an invisible cost becomes a visible, reversible commitment.

Commit once.

At the threshold the ring completes, the index moves by exactly one and the wheel is locked while the panel travels, so trackpad inertia cannot spend a second step.

Roll back.

A second without wheel input drains the ring to zero. Reversing direction starts a fresh sum the other way. At the first and last panel the blocked direction never fills.

Every road in.

Arrow and Page keys, Home and End, a swipe past 55 px, the Prev and Next buttons and the wheel all reach every panel. Each change is announced once. Under reduced motion panels swap in place and the wheel still works.
