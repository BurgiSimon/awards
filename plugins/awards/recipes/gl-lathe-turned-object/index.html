/ gl-lathe-turned-object · dom

/ turned, not downloaded

A spool,
turned in code.

The object is a profile of points spun into a lathe, closed by two ring faces and bored through. Its groove and roughness maps are computed in a worker built from a Blob. Rest a mouse on it for one full turn.

Rules

  • A profile of 48 points, a waist plus a cosine ripple that fades at both ends, goes through LatheGeometry. Two RingGeometry faces and a bore close it.
  • Normal and roughness maps come from one builder function. It runs in a Blob worker and falls back to the main thread when the worker fails or is slow.
  • After a 250 ms dwell, a fine pointer gets one quantised revolution. The turn always ends on a whole turn, and touch input does not start it.
  • Frames render only while the object is turning or after a resize.
