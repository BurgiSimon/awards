Recipe · the close

The last sheet lifts to show who made it.

Scroll to the end. The wordmark slides off the stack and tips on its corner while the footer waits beneath it, held still on a rail. The credits button lifts the whole footer like a sheet on a press bed.

01 · Plates

A footer that is already there.

The footer is a sticky stage inside a rail that starts half a viewport above it. By the time the closing sheet reaches the middle of the screen the stage has stopped moving, so the sheet slides off something that stays put instead of two layers scrolling together.

02 · Editions

Credits live under the footer, not on a page.

The colophon sits beneath the stage. Opening it scrolls to the end while it lifts and tilts the stage on one transition so the credits show in the gap. Escape, the close button or any scroll puts the sheet back down.
