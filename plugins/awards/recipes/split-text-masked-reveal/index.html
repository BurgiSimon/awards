/ split-text-masked-reveal

/ lines

Lines rise out of a mask, one after another, after the fonts have landed.

Split into lines only once document.fonts.ready resolves, re-split on resize by autoSplit, and the reveal is rebuilt inside onSplit so a reflow never leaves half a paragraph hidden.

/ words

Words can stagger too, but lines are the house move.

Per-word reveals are for short headlines; per-character ones are for a logo, never for a paragraph. Below the fold each reveal fires once, on entry.

/ reduced motion

Under reduced motion the text simply appears.

No translate, no mask, a short opacity fade; and the split is reverted so assistive technology reads whole sentences.
