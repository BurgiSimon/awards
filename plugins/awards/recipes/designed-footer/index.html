Alder Workshop / 001

A place for everyday life.

The Alder bench is an imagined piece of furniture built around a simple idea: make it useful, make it legible, and leave room for it to grow familiar.

Explore the study ↘

01 / Materials

Made for the ordinary.

Pale white oak, softened edges and visible pins give the bench its character. Its quiet form lets the material take its own place in a room.

03 / Care

Keep it in use.

Wipe with a soft damp cloth, then dry. Refresh the oil as the surface asks for it. The visible joints are part of the proposal: a worn part should be understood and repaired.
