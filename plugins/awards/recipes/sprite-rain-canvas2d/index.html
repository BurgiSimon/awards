/ sprite-rain-canvas2d

/ easter egg

Press it,
it pours.

A single button lets a shower of drawn sparks fall through the page on one canvas: gravity, a sideways drift and a spin per sprite, capped by the device's quality tier. This page and its copy are a synthetic demo.

Decorative. Off under reduced motion.

/ 01

One canvas, one clock.

Every sprite is a few numbers in an array, drawn from one pre-rendered bitmap with a rotation. The shared ticker drives it and is released as soon as the last sprite leaves the screen.

/ 02

The tier sets the count.

The live sprite count never exceeds the budget from the quality probe. A slow device gets a lighter shower, not a stutter.
