/ gl-endless-reel-sheets · dom

Eight works on an endless reel.

Wheel, swipe or use the arrow keys: the reel wraps, so the first work follows the last. The sheets twist and fold while they travel and lie flat at rest. Every work, its studio and its year are a synthetic demo.
