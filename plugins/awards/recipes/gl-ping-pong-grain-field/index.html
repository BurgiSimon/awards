/ gl-ping-pong-grain-field · dom

/ synthetic demo field

Brush the grains.
The words stay put.

Every grain keeps its own displacement and velocity in a float texture that is read and rewritten each frame. The block you are reading is drawn into a protect mask, so the brush has no force here.

Rules

  • State lives in a ping-pong pair: displacement in RG, velocity in BA, in CSS pixels. Targets try RGBA32F, then RGBA16F, then RGBA8 with 16-bit packed displacement and no inertia.
  • The brush is a capsule from the last pointer position to the current one, with a 1 − r³ falloff. The pointer delta is clamped to 24 px per frame, and a per-grain hash scatters it.
  • The protect mask is drawn from this copy block's rectangle and scales the force to zero inside it.
  • Grains are coloured from the generated surface at their home. A second points pass paints the under-layer at the home of every grain that has moved.
  • Reset glides every grain home over 1.6 s, then clears both targets. The loop stops once the field is at rest.
  • Reduced motion: no grain flies; the brush only reveals the under-layer in place. Static: the surface is drawn once.
