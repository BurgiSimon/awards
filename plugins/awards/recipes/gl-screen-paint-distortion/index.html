gl-screen-paint-distortion · dom

Pointer paint, no solver

Drag a brush
through the glass.

The pointer paints a stroke into a quarter-resolution field. The field advects itself along a blurred copy of its own velocity and curls through gradient noise; one post pass smears the hero along it and fringes the colour where the stroke thins out.

Rules

  • The stroke is a capsule from the last pointer position to this one, so a fast flick leaves a continuous mark, not dots.
  • Its radius grows with the distance moved in a frame (0 to 100 px over 0 to 100 px), so a resting pointer paints nothing.
  • Velocity is stored around 0.5 in an 8-bit target; every weight decays by at least 0.004 a frame, so the field always returns to exactly zero.
  • On a touch screen the brush paints only while a finger is down. Reduced motion: no field and no canvas; the hero is plain DOM.
