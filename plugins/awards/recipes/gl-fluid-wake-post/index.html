/ gl-fluid-wake-post · dom

/ one global layer

The whole page
ripples in your wake.

A 128 × 128 ping-pong velocity field is advected and dissipated every frame and splatted where the pointer moves. One full-frame pass reads it to distort the rendered hero and fringe its channels. There is no second trick; this is the trick.

Rules

  • Advection only: no pressure solve. Dissipation 0.96 per frame keeps the wake short.
  • Splat force is the pointer delta × 10; the splat variance is 0.0015 at rest and 0.002 while moving.
  • The composite pass offsets the UV by the velocity and samples R and B at ± the velocity for the fringe.
  • Half-float render targets when the device has them, bytes with a dead zone otherwise; DPR from the quality tier; everything disposed on pagehide.
  • Reduced motion: no simulation, no canvas; the hero is plain DOM.
