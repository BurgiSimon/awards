A Window for Hale Chapel

A clear east window for a chapel that had lost its stained glass, leaded in the old pattern but glazed with seeded glass that keeps the field outside in view.

Client
Hale Parish
Pieces
212 quarries, 5 lights
Glass
Seeded clear, mouth-blown

Every quarry was cut to the old lead lines traced from the frame, so the window reads as the chapel's own and not as a replacement. Arrive here from the previous case and the plate above is the same element that was playing in its footer.
