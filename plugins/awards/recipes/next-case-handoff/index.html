The Harbour Lamps

Forty hand-blown lamp shades for a harbour wall, each one cut to throw its light down onto the steps and never out to sea.

Client
Harbour Board
Pieces
40 shades, 3 colours
Glass
Soda-lime, sand-cast lips

The shades were blown into one mould and cut by hand at three heights, so the pools of light step down the wall with the tide marks. The plate above is a canvas standing in for the film of the furnace; it keeps playing when you follow the link below.
