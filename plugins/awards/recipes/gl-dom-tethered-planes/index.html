Planes over
real images.

Every image below stays an <img> with alt text. One canvas, positioned absolutely and re-offset to the scroll each frame, draws a plane exactly over each image's rectangle and bends it with the scroll velocity. No WebGL, or reduced motion, and the images are simply images.

Rules

  • The canvas is position: absolute at the top of the document and translated to scrollY every frame: no fixed canvas that drifts against a smooth scroller, no scroll hijack.
  • Each frame reads getBoundingClientRect() of every visible image and writes the plane's position and scale in pixel units through a perspective camera whose distance makes one unit one pixel at depth zero.
  • The bulge is the Floema formula: z −= (sin(uv.y·π) + sin(uv.x·π)) · |speed| · strength, with speed damped from the scroll velocity.
  • DPR from the quality tier, geometry segments from the tier, textures disposed with the renderer on pagehide.
