/ uniforms on the house clock

Tween the
shader, not a loop.

Every shader value here is a GSAP tween target: the charge under the pointer, the swell of the rings and the scene's post look. Each retarget kills the running tween first and starts from where the value is, so a half-finished hover turns around without a jump.
