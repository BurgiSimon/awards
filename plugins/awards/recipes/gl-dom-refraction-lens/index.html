A glass that reads the page

READ IT THROUGH THE GLASS

The page is the picture

Around the glass, the words and hairlines of the page are painted again into a small square canvas. The glass bends that canvas through its front and back faces, so what you see inside it is the page itself, split a breath into colour at the edges.

It goes where the reading is

Each section names a place for the glass. The section nearest the middle of the screen weighs most, and the glass follows the weighted target on a stiff spring, so it arrives without drifting past.
