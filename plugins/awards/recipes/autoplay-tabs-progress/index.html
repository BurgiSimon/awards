Four steps,
one clock.

The tab set below advances on its own, and the bar under the open tab is the timer. It waits until it is on screen, holds while you point at it or move through it with the keyboard, and starts over when you choose a tab. This page and its workshop are a synthetic demo.

How a sash window comes back.

01

Every sash is measured in place. We log the glass, putty and paint of each pane before a single bead comes off.

02

Old crown glass comes out whole whenever it can. Nothing is thrown away before it has been photographed and numbered.

03

Rotten timber is spliced rather than replaced, and loose joints are re-pegged by hand with oak dowels.

04

The sash goes back on new cords, weighted so it holds at any height without a latch.

After the tabs.

Scroll the set off screen and its clock stops; bring it back and it carries on from where it was.
