/ svg-noise-line-field

/ field

Lines that
drift and give.

Every point on every line takes its angle from one noise sample drifting in time. A pointer pushes the points it passes and a spring brings them home. Under reduced motion the field is drawn once and holds still. This hero and its copy are a synthetic demo.

Rules

  • One Perlin sample per point per frame: angle = noise(x, y, t) × 12, offset cos × 32, sin × 16 px.
  • The pointer pushes points within max(175, pointer speed) px; a spring of .005 with damping .925 returns them, scaled to real frame time.
  • Lines draw in from their ends on load, outer lines first, 3 s expo-out with a .5 s spread.
  • Every line is a subpath of one <path>: one draw per frame.
  • Touch devices get fewer points and no push; reduced motion and the static tier get a still field and no ticker.
