Ash Glaze

wood ash · cone 10

Rules

  • The row is a float index. A spring (frequency 9.5, damping 12) pulls it to the nearest whole plate; depth, scale and yaw are read from each plate's distance to it.
  • A wheel gesture becomes one step: deltas under a noise floor are dropped, each is capped, and a threshold fires once. A quiet gap re-arms it; a held, non-decaying gesture repeats after 480 ms.
  • At either end the wheel is released to the page, so the gallery never traps a trackpad.
  • A drag moves the index directly, coasts with friction on release, then hands over to the spring.
  • Arrow keys, Home, End and the buttons step it; an announcer reads the position. Reduced motion snaps directly.
