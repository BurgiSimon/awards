11 skills

Skills cannot call each other, so each phase ends with a written hand-off to the next: an instruction to invoke the next skill, and the file it should read. Every skill also answers its own kind of request on its own. Claude Code writes them /awards:<name>; Codex writes $awards:<name>.

craft
Builds a whole site end to end and owns AWARDS.md; the entry point when you are unsure which skill applies.
concept
Finds the one idea, the story and the signature interaction; deals three directions with a seeded roll and writes the direction contract.
system
Type contract, colour strategy, tokens and themed browser surfaces; writes DESIGN.md and tokens.css.
structure
Page map, hero archetype, components, the semantic skeleton, the responsive strategy and the 404.
stack
Scaffolds Vite (the default), Next, Nuxt, Astro, SvelteKit or a Webflow shell with Lenis and GSAP, and Three when needed, on one ticker.
motion
The motion score: preloader, reveals, scrubs, cursor, transitions and the reduced-motion tiers.
webgl
The GL layer with Three.js, OGL or React Three Fiber: shaders, the asset pipeline, quality tiers and fallbacks.
component
One award-level component inside an existing site, inheriting its world.
jury
Scores work like a design-award jury in a fresh context and returns ordered fixes and a disposition.
ship
Applies the fixes in one batch, then runs the audit, captures, performance, accessibility and meta checks, and writes the ship report.
research
Turns a reference site into a case-study card in the corpus format.

The craft flow

Brief, concept, system, structure, stack, motion, WebGL, jury, ship. Two rules hold throughout: no page code before the direction contract is locked, and no stack chosen before the concept. A phase runs only when its entry condition holds, and its exit file is what the next phase reads.

The direction contract has eight blocks, then a finish line and the seed: THESIS, WORLD, STORY, FIRST VIEWPORT, SIGNATURE, SCROLL MODEL, LOAD & CLOSE and DIVERGENCE. DIVERGENCE names three neighbouring site cards, the principle taken from each and the literal move refused. A mood word in any block, such as premium, immersive or cinematic, sends it back to concept.

The structure phase builds the hero first and judges its capture before anything else exists; the stack phase boots the project with a hook, window.__awards, that lets the capture script and the jury drive the page to any state.

Modes

--quick
For one landing page with one action and its 404. The same contract and every decision the jury scores; system and structure run as one, one jury round and one ship pass.
redesign <url or folder>
For an existing site: a branch first, a baseline capture and critique of the old site, an inventory of what is kept, rewritten or dropped, and the old site's faults written into the new contract as refusals.
programs:
Name the award programs in the brief, for example programs: awwwards, cssda, and the jury reports a target line for each. The scores stay on one rubric; a program changes the target, never the scores.
Deep modes
motion --gsap, webgl --shader and webgl --3d switch on from the contract or by flag, are recorded in AWARDS.md, and load their own playbooks.

Jury

The juror runs in a forked context so the build conversation cannot anchor it. It describes the first viewport in its own words before it reads the contract, walks the page as a visitor would, then scores four axes on anchored scales plus six developer criteria. It never edits the build, and its reply ends on one literal line, disposition: followed by one word.

Jury axes and weights
AxisWeight
Design40 %
Usability30 %
Creativity20 %
Content10 %
ship
Weighted 7.2 or higher, no axis under 6.8, no P0 or P1 audit finding, every contract row kept, and the usability walk passed.
fix
Weighted 6.8 to 7.19, or any axis under 6.8, or a P0 or P1 finding, or a contract block not kept.
rebuild
Weighted under 6.8, a source site a juror could name, a memory test that returns only a mood, or a thesis not visible in the first viewport.
recapture
The evidence is missing, blank, stale or broken. No score exists yet.

The juror scores about 0.7 below the published award on the same sites, by design. When two or more slop-scan families remain on a page, Creativity is capped at 6.

Audit

89 rules in eight families, 27 of them at P0 or P1. Static by default; --render opens the page in Chromium at desktop, tablet and phone widths and adds the checks only a browser can make. Exit code 2 means P0 or P1 findings; a failed browser launch exits 3.

Corpus

62 site cards and 26 pattern files. Cards are read for their principles and their take and don't-take lists; the rest is evidence, never a plan. 55 cards carry a measured composition skeleton: where the first viewport's blocks sit at 1440 and 390 pixels, with no pixels, words or colours of the site.

Every fact on a card carries a label: verified, recalled, inferred or unknown. Site copy is quoted in fragments of at most 25 words, and the corpus captures are other people's work, kept as evidence and never published.

Scripts

Paths are relative to the plugin folder. The skills call these for you; each also runs on its own.

scripts/doctor.mjs <project>
Checks Node, dependencies, Playwright and a real browser launch. Installs nothing.
scripts/audit.mjs <dir|file|url> [--render] [--json]
The craft-floor audit.
scripts/capture.mjs <url|file|dir> [--states <json>]
Jury evidence: scroll positions at desktop, tablet and phone, accessibility trees, reduced motion and scripted states; held-clock timelines, video, throttled and hardware-GPU passes on request.
scripts/new-project.mjs --stack vite --name <dir>
Copies a scaffold with one ticker and the capture hook already wired; Next, Nuxt, Astro and SvelteKit as well.
scripts/roll.mjs --deal 3 --of 7
The seeded direction roll the concept skill uses.
scripts/fonts.mjs search | specimen | fetch
Faces chosen by character from the open Fontsource catalogue: a seeded pull, a rendered specimen of your own headline, then the files and their licence.
scripts/design-md.mjs lint | export
DESIGN.md through Google's design.md tools: lint it, or export its tokens to DTCG or Tailwind.
scripts/assets.mjs add | check | credits
The project's asset and licence log.
scripts/verify-recipes.mjs [--only <ids>]
Builds every recipe and runs its verifier in headless Chromium.
scripts/crux.mjs <url>
Chrome UX Report field data for a live site.
